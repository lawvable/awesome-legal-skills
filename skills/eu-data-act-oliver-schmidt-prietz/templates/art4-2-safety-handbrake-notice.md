# Template: Art. 4(2) safety/security restriction notice

Produces a bilateral notice between the data holder and the user that, by contract, restricts or prohibits the accessing, using or further sharing of certain data on the basis of the safety/security handbrake under Article 4(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act). Includes the parallel Art. 37 notification to the competent authority where the data holder refuses to share data on this ground.

Form: dual-output. (i) A short bilateral notice or contract amendment, and (ii) a competent authority notification letter. Both are required where the data holder relies on Art. 4(2) to refuse data sharing. Markdown by default.

Use this template when the data is potentially restrictable on the basis that processing could undermine security requirements of the connected product, as laid down in Union or national law, resulting in a serious adverse effect on the health, safety or security of natural persons. Do NOT use this template as a substitute for the trade-secret handbrake under Art. 4(6)-(8); the regimes are distinct.

---

## Part 1: Bilateral notice or contract amendment

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [User, full legal entity name / natural person name and
     address]

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to any underlying user
            request]

Subject: Notice of contractual restriction on data access, use or
         further sharing pursuant to Article 4(2) of Regulation
         (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Background and scope

   This notice concerns the connected product(s) [identifier(s)]
   and the related service(s) [identifier(s)] under the [contract
   reference] between [data holder] and [user].

   [Where applicable, summary of the underlying data request
   under Art. 4(1) that has prompted this notice; or, where the
   restriction is being agreed pre-contractually under Art. 3,
   summary of the data categories covered.]

2. The Art. 4(2) basis for the restriction

   The data holder is relying on Art. 4(2) of the Data Act, which
   provides:

   > "Users and data holders may contractually restrict or
   > prohibit accessing, using or further sharing data, if such
   > processing could undermine security requirements of the
   > connected product, as laid down by Union or national law,
   > resulting in a serious adverse effect on the health, safety
   > or security of natural persons."

   The two operative conditions are:

   - (a) The processing could undermine security requirements of
     the connected product, as laid down by Union or national
     law.

   - (b) That undermining would result in a serious adverse
     effect on the health, safety or security of natural persons.

3. Identification of the security requirements

   The security requirements of the connected product engaged by
   this restriction are laid down in the following Union or
   national law instruments: [enumerated instruments, with
   specific provisions cited. Examples: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847
   (Cyber Resilience Act) Annex I and III essential requirements
   for the connected product; Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)
   technical and organisational measures for [covered category];
   Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 (General Safety Regulation) for
   vehicles; Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (Medical Devices
   Regulation) for medical devices; specified national security
   law instrument [identification]].

4. Identification of the serious adverse effect

   [Specific factual demonstration that the data access, use or
   sharing in question could result in a serious adverse effect
   on the health, safety or security of natural persons. The
   demonstration should connect the security requirement
   identified in section 3 to the specific data and the specific
   scenario.]

   [Where sectoral authorities have provided technical expertise
   under Art. 4(2) second sentence: "The data holder has
   consulted [sectoral authority] under the second sentence of
   Art. 4(2), which has provided technical expertise on the
   safety/security implications of the requested access on
   [date]. A summary of the expertise relied on is enclosed."]

5. Scope of the restriction

   The data holder and the user hereby agree that:

   [Specific contractual restriction or prohibition. Examples:

   - The user shall not access [specific data fields / categories]
     in real-time during [operational phase], because such access
     would [specific safety/security mechanism].
   - The user may access [specific data fields / categories] only
     in [anonymised / aggregated / delayed] form, because direct
     real-time disclosure would [specific safety/security
     mechanism].
   - The user shall not further share [specific data fields /
     categories] with third parties, because such sharing would
     [specific safety/security mechanism].]

   The restriction is no broader than necessary to address the
   identified serious adverse effect. Data not covered by this
   restriction continues to be subject to the data holder's Art.
   4(1) obligations.

6. Duration and review

   This restriction is in force from [start date] and remains in
   force until [end date or "until the security requirements
   identified in section 3 are amended or the serious adverse
   effect identified in section 4 ceases to apply"]. The data
   holder and the user agree to review the restriction
   [periodically, on at least [number]-monthly cadence / upon any
   material change in the connected product, the related
   service, or the underlying security requirement].

7. Redress

   In any dispute between the user and the data holder concerning
   this contractual restriction, the user may, without prejudice
   to its right to seek redress before a court or tribunal of a
   Member State:
   (a) lodge a complaint with the competent authority designated
       pursuant to Art. 37 of the Data Act in the Member State of
       [data holder's establishment], in accordance with Art. 4(3)
       and Art. 37(5)(b); or
   (b) agree with the data holder to refer the matter to a
       dispute settlement body in accordance with Art. 10(1).

8. Competent authority notification

   The data holder has, in parallel with this notice, notified
   the competent authority of [Member State of establishment] of
   the refusal to share data on the basis of Art. 4(2), pursuant
   to Art. 4(2) third sentence.

[Signature block placeholder, both parties]
```

## Part 2: Competent authority notification letter

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [Competent authority designated pursuant to Article 37 of
     the Data Act in the Member State of establishment, full
     name and address]
    [Where the Member State has designated a data coordinator
    pursuant to Art. 37(2), address the notification to the data
    coordinator.]

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference]

Subject: Notification of refusal to share data pursuant to
         Article 4(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Notifying entity

   [Data holder, full legal entity name, registered address,
   contact point under Art. 37 if any.]

2. Refusal context

   The data holder has, by notice to [user] dated [date], applied
   a contractual restriction on the accessing, using or further
   sharing of data pursuant to Art. 4(2) of the Data Act. A copy
   of the bilateral notice is enclosed.

3. Identification of the security requirement and the serious
   adverse effect

   The security requirements of the connected product engaged by
   the restriction are: [enumerated instruments, with provisions].
   The serious adverse effect on the health, safety or security
   of natural persons that processing without the restriction
   could result in is: [specific factual demonstration; same as
   in Part 1 section 4].

4. Scope and duration of the restriction

   [Summary; cross-reference to the bilateral notice for the full
   text.]

5. User information

   The user against whom the restriction is in force is [name,
   contact details if appropriate]. The user has been informed of
   its redress options under Art. 4(3) and Art. 37(5)(b).

6. Sectoral authority expertise (where applicable)

   [Where the data holder has consulted a sectoral authority
   under Art. 4(2) second sentence: identification of the
   sectoral authority and brief reference to the expertise
   relied on.]

[Signature block placeholder]

Enclosures:
- Copy of the bilateral notice to the user dated [date]
- Where applicable: copy of the sectoral authority's technical
  expertise
```
