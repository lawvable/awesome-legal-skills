# Template: Art. 17 public sector body request

Produces a public sector body's (or the Commission's, the European Central Bank's, or a Union body's) written request to a data holder for data on the basis of an exceptional need under Article 17 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act). Covers the Art. 17(1) content requirements and the Art. 17(2) format and procedural requirements.

Form: formal request. Markdown by default. Length typically two to four pages depending on the scope of the request and the demonstration of exceptional need.

PRECONDITION: An exceptional need within the meaning of Art. 15(1) is the substantive prerequisite. The request must demonstrate the need; without it, the Art. 18(2)(c) ground for decline applies. Where the need is under Art. 15(1)(b), Art. 15(2) excludes microenterprises and small enterprises from the obligation.

PRECONDITION: Art. 16(2) excludes activities for the prevention, investigation, detection or prosecution of criminal or administrative offences, the execution of criminal penalties, customs, and taxation administration. Where the request is for those activities, Art. 17 is the wrong instrument.

```
[Requesting body letterhead placeholder]

To: [Data holder, full legal entity name and registered address]

Date: [Date of request]

Reference: [Internal reference number of the requesting body]

Subject: Request for data on the basis of an exceptional need
         pursuant to Article 17 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854
         (Data Act)

1. Identity of the requesting body

   The requesting body is [public sector body / the Commission /
   the European Central Bank / Union body, full name and
   address]. The contact point for this request is [name,
   position, contact details].

   [Where the request is by a public sector body, identify the
   Member State of establishment.]

2. Data requested (Art. 17(1)(a))

   The data requested, including the relevant metadata necessary
   to interpret and use the data, is:

   [Specific identification of the data: category, fields, time
   window, granularity, format. The requesting body must specify
   the data required.]

3. Demonstration of exceptional need (Art. 17(1)(b))

   The conditions for an exceptional need under Art. 15 are met
   as follows:

   [Identify which Art. 15(1) ground applies and substantiate
   the conditions specific to that ground.

   Art. 15(1)(a): public emergency.

   - The matter is a public emergency. The relevant public
     emergency is [specific identification: nature, geographic
     scope, timeframe, the public-authority declaration or
     equivalent basis for treating the matter as a public
     emergency].

   - The requesting body is unable to obtain the data by
     alternative means in a timely and effective manner under
     equivalent conditions. The alternative means considered and
     why they are inadequate are: [specific account: e.g. no
     dataset of equivalent quality exists in the public domain;
     market purchase is not available at the required latency;
     existing reporting obligations of other entities do not
     cover the needed scope.]

   Art. 15(1)(b): non-emergency specific public-interest task,
   non-personal data only.

   - The requesting body is acting on the basis of [Union law
     / national law instrument; specific provision].

   - The specific public-interest task is [identification,
     explicitly provided for by law].

   - The lack of the requested data prevents the requesting
     body from fulfilling that task because [specific account].

   - The requesting body has exhausted all other means at its
     disposal to obtain the data, including: [identification of
     the means and why each was unsuccessful or inadequate.
     Include any market purchase attempts at market rates and
     reasons they did not succeed.]

   - Microenterprise and small-enterprise carve-out: under Art.
     15(2), Art. 15(1)(b) does not apply to microenterprises
     and small enterprises. The data holder is [not / is] within
     this carve-out [identification].]

4. Purpose, intended use, duration (Art. 17(1)(c))

   The purpose of this request is [statement of purpose tied to
   the Art. 15 ground].

   The intended use of the data is [specific use, including any
   intended sharing with third parties under Art. 17(4) or with
   research organisations or statistical bodies under Art. 21].

   The expected duration of use is [period or "until the
   specific task is fulfilled, expected to be [period]"]. Where
   personal data is requested, the processing addresses the
   exceptional need as follows: [specific account].

5. Expected erasure (Art. 17(1)(d))

   The requesting body expects to erase the data, and to require
   any third party with access under Art. 17(4) or Art. 21 to
   erase the data, by [date or trigger]. Erasure will be
   notified to the data holder and to any individuals or
   organisations that received the data pursuant to Art. 21(1),
   in accordance with Art. 19(1)(c).

6. Justification of the choice of data holder (Art. 17(1)(e))

   The data holder addressed by this request was selected on the
   following basis: [specific factual reasons for selecting this
   data holder rather than others; ideally substantiated by
   characteristics of the data holder's data, market position,
   prior cooperation, or technical accessibility.]

7. Other recipients (Art. 17(1)(f))

   The requesting body anticipates sharing the data requested
   with the following other public sector bodies, the
   Commission, the European Central Bank, Union bodies, or third
   parties:

   [Identification or "none expected at this time."]

   Where third parties are anticipated, they include: [identify
   third parties to which functions have been delegated under
   Art. 17(4) by means of a publicly available agreement; or
   research organisations and statistical bodies under Art.
   21.]

8. Personal data safeguards (Art. 17(1)(g))

   [Where personal data is requested:]

   The technical and organisational measures necessary and
   proportionate to implement data protection principles and
   necessary safeguards are:

   [Specific identification: pseudonymisation, encryption,
   access restrictions, retention controls, security incident
   response.]

   The requesting body has assessed whether anonymisation can
   be applied by the data holder before making the data
   available. The assessment is: [anonymisation is feasible and
   the data holder is requested to anonymise; or anonymisation
   is not feasible because the exceptional need requires
   personal-data granularity, and the data is requested in
   pseudonymised form in accordance with Art. 17(2)(e)].

   The supervisory authority responsible for monitoring the
   GDPR in the Member State where the requesting body is
   established has been notified without undue delay, in
   accordance with Art. 17(2)(i). Notification was made on
   [date] under reference [reference].

   [Where the request does not concern personal data: "This
   request does not concern personal data. The data holder is
   requested to confirm if it disagrees with that
   characterisation."]

9. Legal basis for the task (Art. 17(1)(h))

   The legal provision allocating to the requesting body the
   specific task carried out in the public interest relevant to
   this request is: [specific citation: Article number,
   regulation or directive, or national law provision].

10. Deadlines (Art. 17(1)(i))

    The requesting body requests delivery of the data by [date].
    Under Art. 18(2), the data holder may decline or seek
    modification of the request without undue delay and, in any
    event, no later than:
    (a) [where Art. 15(1)(a) ground] five working days after
        receipt of this request; or
    (b) [where Art. 15(1)(b) ground] thirty working days after
        receipt of this request.

11. Avoidance of compliance-induced liability (Art. 17(1)(j))

    The requesting body has made best efforts to avoid the
    compliance with this request resulting in the data holder's
    liability for infringement of Union or national law. The
    following considerations have been taken into account:
    [specific account: e.g. GDPR compatibility; sectoral law
    compatibility; trade-secret regime compatibility under Art.
    19(3)].

12. Form and language (Art. 17(2)(a))

    This request is made in writing, in clear, concise and plain
    language understandable to the data holder.

13. Proportionality (Art. 17(2)(c))

    This request is proportionate to the exceptional need and
    duly justified. The granularity and volume of data requested
    and the frequency of access are calibrated as follows:
    [specific account: only the data necessary to fulfil the
    exceptional need; access frequency limited to what the task
    requires; format selected to minimise transformation burden
    on the data holder.]

14. Trade-secret protection (Art. 17(2)(d), Art. 19(3))

    The requesting body commits to ensuring the protection of
    trade secrets in accordance with Art. 19(3) of the Data Act.
    Where the data holder identifies any of the requested data
    as trade-secret-protected, the requesting body will, prior
    to disclosure, take all necessary and appropriate technical
    and organisational measures to preserve the confidentiality
    of the trade secrets. The requesting body invites the data
    holder to identify trade-secret-protected data and to
    indicate proposed safeguards.

15. Personal-data-only-where-necessary (Art. 17(2)(e))

    [Repeated only where personal data is requested.]

    The requesting body has demonstrated that non-personal data
    alone is insufficient to respond to the exceptional need.
    Where personal data is requested under Art. 15(1)(a), the
    request is for data in pseudonymised form and the technical
    and organisational measures are established as set out in
    section 8.

16. Penalties (Art. 17(2)(f))

    The data holder is informed that, in the event of non-
    compliance with this request, the competent authority
    designated pursuant to Art. 37 may impose penalties under
    Art. 40.

17. Public availability (Art. 17(2)(g), (h))

    [Where the requesting body is a public sector body of a
    Member State: "This request will be transmitted to the data
    coordinator referred to in Art. 37 of [Member State], who
    will make the request publicly available online without
    undue delay, subject to the data coordinator's assessment
    of any risk to public security under Art. 17(2)(g)."]

    [Where the requesting body is the Commission, the ECB, or
    a Union body: "This request will be made available online
    without undue delay, in accordance with Art. 17(2)(h)."]

18. Decline and modification

    The data holder may decline or seek modification of this
    request under Art. 18(2) within the timelines stated in
    section 10, on the grounds listed in Art. 18(2)(a)-(c).

19. Cross-border note

    [Where the data holder is established in a different Member
    State from the requesting public sector body, or where the
    requesting body is the Commission, ECB or Union body: "In
    accordance with Art. 22(3), the requesting body has notified
    the competent authority of the Member State of [data
    holder's establishment] of the intention to make this
    request. The request is being examined by that competent
    authority and is being communicated to the data holder
    pursuant to Art. 22(4)(a)."]

[Signature block placeholder]

Enclosures (where applicable):
- Where personal data is requested: notification to the GDPR
  supervisory authority under Art. 17(2)(i).
- Where applicable: the publicly available delegation agreement
  with any third party under Art. 17(4).
- Where applicable: the data coordinator's confirmation of
  online publication or risk assessment under Art. 17(2)(g).
```
