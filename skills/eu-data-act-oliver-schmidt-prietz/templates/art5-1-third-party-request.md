# Template: User-directed Art. 5(1) third-party data request

Produces a written user instruction to the data holder to make IoT product and related service data available to a named third party under Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act).

Form: short formal letter, jointly signed or expressly authorised by the user. Markdown by default. One page typical.

---

```
[User or acting party letterhead placeholder]

To: [Data holder, full legal entity name and registered address]

Date: [Date of request]

Subject: Request to make data available to a third party
         pursuant to Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854
         (Data Act)

1. Identity of the requesting user and the designated third party

   The user is [full legal entity name / natural person name],
   [address]. The user is [the user of / the renter or lessee of /
   the owner of] [connected product identifier(s)] [where
   applicable: "and the subscriber to [related service(s)"].

   The user designates [third party, full legal entity name and
   registered address] as the recipient of the data identified in
   section 2 below ("the Third Party").

   [Option, where a party acts on behalf of the user under Art. 5(1)
   first sentence: "[Acting party] is submitting this request on
   behalf of the user, pursuant to a mandate dated [date], a copy of
   which is enclosed."]

   The user confirms that the Third Party is not an undertaking
   designated as a gatekeeper pursuant to Article 3 of Regulation
   (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act). [Where the Third Party's
   gatekeeper status is uncertain: "The user has verified the
   Commission's current list of designated gatekeepers as at [date]
   and the Third Party is not on that list."]

2. Data requested

   [Specific description of the data to be made available to the
   Third Party. Identify by category, by data field where known, and
   by time window. The request covers the data and the relevant
   metadata necessary to interpret and use the data, in accordance
   with Art. 5(1).]

3. Legal basis

   This request is made under Art. 5(1) of the Data Act. Upon
   request by a user, or by a party acting on behalf of a user, the
   data holder is required to make readily available data, with the
   relevant metadata necessary to interpret and use the data,
   available to a third party without undue delay, of the same
   quality as is available to the data holder, easily, securely,
   free of charge to the user, in a comprehensive, structured,
   commonly used and machine-readable format and, where relevant and
   technically feasible, continuously and in real-time. Compensation
   between the data holder and the Third Party is governed by Arts.
   8 and 9.

4. Purpose agreed with the Third Party

   The user and the Third Party have agreed that the Third Party
   will use the data for the following purpose(s): [specific
   purpose description]. The Third Party is aware of and bound by
   the restrictions in Art. 6, including the closed list of
   prohibitions in Art. 6(2).

5. Format and delivery

   The user requests delivery [via secure file transfer to the
   Third Party's endpoint at [endpoint] / via API access at
   [endpoint] / via the data holder's published portal]. [Where
   continuous or real-time delivery is technically feasible: "The
   user further requests that the data be made available
   continuously and in real-time from [date] onwards."]

6. Trade-secret stage-1 engagement

   The user acknowledges that some of the data may be identified by
   the data holder as trade-secret-protected. The Third Party is
   prepared to engage in stage-1 negotiation under Art. 5(9)
   regarding proportionate technical and organisational measures to
   preserve the confidentiality of any such data. The user requests
   that the data holder identify the specific categories it claims
   as trade-secret-protected and engage with the Third Party
   directly on the proposed safeguards within [number] working days
   of receipt of this request.

7. Personal data

   [Option A, where the user is the data subject: "The user is the
   data subject for any personal data within the scope of this
   request. The user instructs the data holder to make such personal
   data available to the Third Party. The user has separately
   informed the Third Party of the purpose and arranged the
   applicable GDPR legal basis for processing by the Third Party."]

   [Option B, where the user is not the data subject and is an
   enterprise: "Personal data within the scope of this request will
   be made available to the Third Party only where there is a valid
   legal basis for processing under Art. 6 of Regulation (EU)
   2016/679 (GDPR) and, where relevant, the conditions of Art. 9 of
   that Regulation and of Art. 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC are
   fulfilled, in accordance with Art. 5(7). The user confirms that
   the user (as controller for the personal data within the meaning
   of Recital 34) has satisfied these conditions and has the
   following legal basis: [legal basis with brief substantiation]."]

8. Timeline and response

   The user requests confirmation of receipt within [number] working
   days. Where the data holder anticipates a need to invoke Art.
   5(9) (trade-secret safeguards), Art. 4(2) (security restriction,
   applicable through the Ch II framework), or any other limit on
   the request, the user requests notification within the same
   period.

9. Redress reservation

   This request is made without prejudice to the user's and the
   Third Party's rights to lodge a complaint with the competent
   authority designated pursuant to Art. 37 of the Data Act and to
   seek redress before a court or tribunal of a Member State.

[Signature: user or, where applicable, acting party authorised by
the user, with the user's countersignature]

Enclosures (where applicable):
- Mandate from the user to the acting party
- Confirmation by the Third Party of its undertaking to comply with
  Art. 6 obligations (recommended; not required by the regulation)
- Proof of user status
```
