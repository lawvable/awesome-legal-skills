# Template: Art. 9 compensation statement

Produces the data holder's written compensation statement under Article 9 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act). The statement sets out the basis for the compensation the data holder seeks from the data recipient in a B2B data-sharing arrangement under Chapter III. Required by Art. 9(7) on the recipient's request.

Form: written statement, addressed to the data recipient, suitable as a stand-alone explanation or as an annex to a data-sharing contract. Markdown by default. Length typically two to three pages.

Use this template wherever the data holder makes data available to a data recipient under Art. 5 or other applicable Union law mandating data sharing, and the recipient has asked the data holder to provide the calculation basis under Art. 9(7).

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [Data recipient, full legal entity name and registered
     address]

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to the underlying data-
            sharing arrangement and to the recipient's request for
            this statement, where applicable]

Subject: Compensation statement under Article 9 of Regulation
         (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Background and scope

   This statement sets out the basis for the compensation the
   Data Holder seeks from the Recipient for making available the
   data identified in section 2 below, in accordance with Art. 9
   of the Data Act.

   [Identify the data-sharing context. Examples:

   - "The Data Holder is making the data available to the
     Recipient pursuant to a user-directed request under Art.
     5(1) of the Data Act dated [date]. The user is [name]; the
     Recipient is the third party to whom the user has directed
     disclosure."

   - "The Data Holder is making the data available to the
     Recipient pursuant to [identified Union law instrument]
     [e.g. Regulation (EU) [yyyy/xxxx] (sectoral name)] under
     Art. 8(1) of the Data Act."]

2. Data covered

   The data covered by this statement is:

   [Specific identification of the data, by category, by data
   field where known, by time window, and by delivery mode (one-
   time export, continuous feed, API access).]

3. Status of the Recipient

   [Identify the Recipient's relevant status for Art. 9
   purposes. Material categories are:

   (a) SME or not-for-profit research organisation without
       partner or linked enterprises that do not qualify as
       SMEs: Art. 9(4) cap applies; compensation is limited to
       the costs of making the data available (Art. 9(2)(a)
       items only).

   (b) Other recipient: Art. 9(1)-(3) apply; compensation must
       be non-discriminatory and reasonable and may include a
       margin.

   The Data Holder identifies the Recipient as [SME / not-for-
   profit research organisation / other]. Where the Recipient
   has self-identified as falling within Art. 9(4), the basis
   for that identification is: [recipient's representation or
   verification basis].]

4. Compensation structure

   [Where Art. 9(4) cap applies (SME / not-for-profit research
   organisation):]

   In accordance with Art. 9(4) of the Data Act, the
   compensation shall not exceed the costs referred to in Art.
   9(2)(a): the costs incurred in making the data available,
   including, in particular, the costs necessary for the
   formatting of data, dissemination via electronic means and
   storage. Investments in the collection and production of
   data (Art. 9(2)(b)) and any margin are not chargeable to a
   recipient covered by the Art. 9(4) cap.

   [Where Art. 9(4) cap does not apply:]

   In accordance with Art. 9(1)-(3) of the Data Act, the
   compensation is non-discriminatory and reasonable and may
   include a margin. The compensation takes into account:
   (a) the costs incurred in making the data available
       (Art. 9(2)(a));
   (b) investments in the collection and production of data,
       where applicable, taking into account whether other
       parties contributed to obtaining, generating or
       collecting the data in question (Art. 9(2)(b));
   (c) where relevant, the volume, format and nature of the
       data (Art. 9(3)).

5. Calculation: costs (Art. 9(2)(a))

   The costs incurred in making the data available are
   calculated as follows:

   [Itemised. Examples:

   (a) Formatting costs. [Specific items: data transformation;
       schema mapping; format conversion to JSON/CSV/parquet/
       industry-standard; cost basis: hourly rates, vendor
       fees, allocated infrastructure costs. Indicative
       quantification: [amount or method].]

   (b) Dissemination via electronic means. [API hosting; data
       transfer; bandwidth; security infrastructure. Cost
       basis: allocated infrastructure costs; vendor invoices;
       internal cost recovery rates. Indicative quantification:
       [amount or method].]

   (c) Storage. [Data storage allocated to the recipient's
       access window; backup and retention costs. Cost basis:
       allocated infrastructure costs. Indicative
       quantification: [amount or method].]

   (d) [Other costs reasonably attributable to making the data
       available, where applicable. Examples: anonymisation,
       pseudonymisation, aggregation, technical adaptation;
       audit logging infrastructure; security operations
       relating to the trade-secret safeguards under Art.
       5(9).]

   Aggregate Art. 9(2)(a) costs for the data-sharing
   arrangement: [amount, with calculation basis].

6. [Where Art. 9(4) cap does not apply:] Calculation:
   investments (Art. 9(2)(b))

   The Data Holder's investments in the collection and
   production of the data are calculated as follows:

   [Itemised. Identify the relevant investments and the basis
   for attributing a share of those investments to the data
   made available. Examples:

   (a) R&D investment in the connected product whose data is
       being made available. [Reasonably allocable share; not
       the full investment, but the portion attributable to
       the data layer rather than the product itself.]

   (b) Investment in data infrastructure: collection
       infrastructure (sensors, connectivity); data pipeline
       infrastructure (ingestion, ETL, storage); data quality
       and curation processes.

   (c) Where applicable, the contribution of other parties
       (e.g. component suppliers contributing to data
       generation; service partners contributing to data
       enrichment) and the share of investment attributable to
       the Data Holder.

   Aggregate Art. 9(2)(b) attributable investment: [amount,
   with calculation basis].]

7. [Where Art. 9(4) cap does not apply:] Volume, format,
   nature of the data (Art. 9(3))

   [Where the compensation depends on volume, format or
   nature of the data: identify the variable components.
   Examples: per-record pricing for one-time exports; tiered
   pricing for continuous feeds; premium pricing for real-time
   delivery.]

8. [Where Art. 9(4) cap does not apply:] Margin

   The Data Holder applies a margin of [percentage / amount]
   to the cost-plus-investment basis. The margin is calibrated
   to: [reasoned explanation tied to comparable B2B data-
   sharing arrangements, market practice in the sector, the
   Recipient's category, and other reasonable factors.]

9. Non-discrimination (Art. 8(3))

   The Data Holder confirms that the compensation calculation
   set out above is non-discriminatory as between comparable
   categories of data recipients. [Where the Recipient has
   raised a reasoned request under Art. 8(3) second sentence
   regarding potential discrimination, address it specifically
   with the comparison data.]

   The Data Holder does not discriminate in favour of partner
   enterprises or linked enterprises of the Data Holder.

10. Information rights of the Recipient (Art. 9(7))

    Under Art. 9(7), the Recipient may request further detail
    on any item in this statement. The Data Holder will
    respond without undue delay to such requests, subject to
    the confidentiality of any third-party-supplier costs
    referenced.

11. Commission guidelines (Art. 9(5))

    [Where the Commission has adopted guidelines under Art.
    9(5)] The Data Holder has taken into account the
    Commission guidelines on the calculation of reasonable
    compensation dated [date]. The calculation in this
    statement reflects those guidelines. [Where the
    guidelines have not been adopted at the time of this
    statement, omit this section.]

12. Dispute mechanisms

    Disagreements about the compensation calculation may be
    addressed through:
    (a) a reasoned request under Art. 8(3) for non-
        discrimination information;
    (b) reference to a dispute settlement body certified
        under Art. 10(1) of the Data Act in accordance with
        Art. 10(4) (for data processing services) or Art.
        10(1) (for general Ch III disputes);
    (c) complaint to the competent authority designated
        pursuant to Art. 37; or
    (d) the Recipient's right to seek redress before a court
        or tribunal of a Member State.

[Signature block placeholder]

Enclosures:
- [Where applicable: supporting calculation worksheets, vendor
  invoices, internal cost recovery rate schedules, comparable-
  contract evidence on non-discrimination.]
```
