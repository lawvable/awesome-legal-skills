# Template: Art. 18 decline or modify letter

Produces the data holder's written response declining a public sector body's Art. 17 request, or seeking its modification, under Article 18(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act). Must be sent within the 5 or 30 working-day window (5 for Art. 15(1)(a) public emergency requests, 30 for other exceptional-need requests).

Form: formal letter to the requesting body. Markdown by default. Length typically one to two pages.

Use this template where the data holder is declining or modifying on one or more of the three Art. 18(2) grounds:
- (a) the data holder does not have control over the data requested;
- (b) a similar request for the same purpose has been previously submitted by another public sector body or the Commission, the European Central Bank or a Union body, and the data holder has not been notified of the erasure of the data pursuant to Art. 19(1)(c);
- (c) the request does not meet the conditions laid down in Art. 17(1) and (2).

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [Requesting body, full name and address]

Date: [Date of response; no later than the Art. 18(2) deadline,
       which is 5 working days for Art. 15(1)(a) public emergency
       requests and 30 working days for other exceptional-need
       requests]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to the requesting
            body's request and to the requesting body's
            reference number]

Subject: [Decline / request for modification] in response to
         request dated [date] under Article 17 of Regulation
         (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Receipt of the request

   The Data Holder received the request from [requesting body]
   dated [date] on [receipt date]. The request seeks data
   identified as [brief restatement]. The Data Holder has
   considered the request in accordance with Art. 18 of the Data
   Act.

2. Timeliness of this response

   This response is provided within the timeframe specified in
   Art. 18(2):
   [Option A, where the request invokes Art. 15(1)(a) public
   emergency: "Within 5 working days of receipt."]
   [Option B, where the request invokes Art. 15(1)(b): "Within
   30 working days of receipt."]

3. Ground(s) for declining or seeking modification

   The Data Holder relies on the following ground(s) under Art.
   18(2):

   [Pick the ground(s) actually engaged. Do not assert multiple
   grounds unless each is independently engaged.]

   [Option A: Art. 18(2)(a) - no control over the data]

   The Data Holder does not have control over the data requested
   within the meaning of Art. 18(2)(a). Specifically:

   [Factual account: the data is not in the Data Holder's
   possession; the data is held by a different entity within the
   corporate group but that entity is not the Data Holder; the
   data has been deleted in the ordinary course of business
   before the request was received; the data was never
   generated; the Data Holder is not a controller and the data
   is in the possession of [identified third party]. The account
   must be factual; "control" is operative legal control of the
   data, not generalised business relationship.]

   [Option B: Art. 18(2)(b) - prior request, no erasure
   notification]

   A similar request for the same purpose has been previously
   submitted to the Data Holder by [previous requesting body,
   in accordance with Art. 18(3) which requires identification].
   The Data Holder made the data available to [previous
   requesting body] on [date] in response to that request. The
   Data Holder has not been notified of the erasure of the data
   pursuant to Art. 19(1)(c).

   In accordance with Art. 18(3), the Data Holder identifies the
   previously requesting body as: [name and contact].

   [Option C: Art. 18(2)(c) - the request does not meet Art.
   17(1) or 17(2)]

   The request does not meet one or more of the conditions in
   Art. 17(1) and 17(2). Specifically:

   [Specific identification of which conditions are not met, with
   factual basis for each. Examples (only those actually engaged):

   - Art. 17(1)(a): the data required is not sufficiently
     specified. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(b): the conditions of exceptional need under
     Art. 15 are not demonstrated. [Specific deficiency: e.g.
     no public emergency identified; alternative means
     exhaustion not demonstrated; request not within Art.
     15(1)(b) scope because it concerns personal data.]

   - Art. 17(1)(c): the purpose, intended use, duration, or, in
     the case of personal data, the manner of addressing the
     exceptional need is not explained. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(d): the expected erasure timing is not
     specified. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(e): the justification for the choice of this
     Data Holder is not provided. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(f): the other public sector bodies or third
     parties with whom the data will be shared are not
     specified. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(g): for personal data, the necessary technical
     and organisational measures and the anonymisation
     assessment are not provided. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(h): the legal provision allocating the task
     carried out in the public interest is not stated.
     [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(i): the deadline by which the data are to be
     made available or the Art. 18(2) decline window are not
     specified. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(1)(j): the requesting body's best-effort
     compliance-risk-avoidance is not demonstrated. [Specific
     deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(a): not in writing, or not in clear, concise
     and plain language. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(b): not specific regarding the type of data, or
     does not correspond to data the Data Holder controls at
     the time of the request. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(c): not proportionate to the exceptional need;
     not duly justified regarding granularity, volume, and
     frequency. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(d): does not respect the Data Holder's
     legitimate aims, including trade-secret protection under
     Art. 19(3) and the costs/effort of making the data
     available. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(e): personal data requested without
     demonstration that non-personal data is insufficient, or
     not requested in pseudonymised form. [Specific
     deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(g): not transmitted via the data coordinator of
     the relevant Member State (where applicable). [Specific
     deficiency.]

   - Art. 17(2)(i): for personal data, supervisory authority
     notification not made. [Specific deficiency.]

   - Art. 22(3): for cross-border requests, the competent
     authority of the Data Holder's Member State was not
     notified. [Specific deficiency, where applicable.]]

4. Decline or modification

   [Where the response is a decline:]

   For the reason(s) stated in section 3, the Data Holder
   declines the request.

   [Where the response is a request for modification:]

   For the reason(s) stated in section 3, the Data Holder
   cannot comply with the request as submitted. The Data
   Holder would be in a position to comply if the request were
   modified as follows:

   [Specific modifications: identification of the changes that
   would address the deficiencies and on which terms the Data
   Holder could deliver the data.]

   The Data Holder invites the requesting body to consider the
   modifications and to submit a revised request.

5. Trade-secret protection reservation (Art. 19(3))

   [Where the request encompasses data the Data Holder
   identifies as trade-secret-protected, and the request is
   nonetheless complied with after any modification:]

   Where the Data Holder complies, in whole or in part, with
   the (modified) request, the Data Holder identifies the
   trade-secret-protected data within the relevant metadata,
   in accordance with Art. 19(3). The Data Holder requests that
   the requesting body confirm in advance the technical and
   organisational measures it will take prior to disclosure to
   preserve the confidentiality of the trade secrets.

6. Cross-border note (Art. 22)

   [Where applicable:]

   The Data Holder is established in [Member State]. Under Art.
   22(3) and (4), this matter [is being / has been] examined by
   the competent authority of [Member State] designated
   pursuant to Art. 37. The Data Holder copies this response to
   that competent authority.

7. Redress

   The matter may be referred to the competent authority
   designated pursuant to Art. 37 of the Data Act in the Member
   State of [Data Holder's establishment] in accordance with
   Art. 18(5), where the requesting body wishes to challenge
   this response and where the matter cannot be resolved by
   appropriate modification of the request.

[Signature block placeholder]

Enclosures (where applicable):
- Where Art. 18(2)(b) is invoked: confirmation or summary of the
  prior compliance with [previous requesting body].
- Where Art. 18(2)(c) is invoked: where helpful, an annotated
  version of the requesting body's request highlighting the
  specific deficiencies.
```
