# Template: Art. 4(6) trade-secret safeguards agreement (stage 1)

Produces the stage-1 written agreement between the data holder (or the trade secret holder where different) and the user, setting out the proportionate technical and organisational measures necessary to preserve the confidentiality of trade-secret-protected data within the scope of an Art. 4(1) request. Equivalent to the Art. 5(9) safeguards for third-party requests; the same template structure applies with the user replaced by the third party.

Form: standalone agreement, or addendum to the underlying contract or to the data holder's Art. 4(1) response. Markdown by default. Typically two to four pages depending on the number of measures.

---

```
[Data holder letterhead or two-party agreement format]

Stage-1 confidentiality safeguards agreement under Article 4(6) of
Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

Between:
   [Data holder, full legal entity name, registered address] ("the
   Data Holder")
   [Where the trade secret holder is not the same person as the
   data holder: "(the Data Holder, acting also on behalf of [trade
   secret holder, full legal entity name and registered address]
   ('the Trade Secret Holder'))"]

And:
   [User, full legal entity name, registered address] ("the User")

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to the underlying Art.
            4(1) request from the User]

---

1. Background

   The User has, by request dated [date], requested access to data
   from [data holder] pursuant to Art. 4(1) of the Data Act. The
   Data Holder has identified certain of that data as protected by
   trade secret within the meaning of Art. 2(19) Data Act and Art.
   2(1) of Directive (EU) 2016/943 (Trade Secrets Directive). In
   accordance with Art. 4(6), the parties have negotiated the
   following technical and organisational measures necessary to
   preserve the confidentiality of the trade-secret-protected data
   prior to disclosure.

2. Trade-secret data covered by this agreement

   The data identified by the Data Holder (or, where applicable,
   by the Trade Secret Holder) as trade-secret-protected within
   the scope of the User's request is:

   [Category-by-category or field-by-field identification. The
   identification is also reflected in the relevant metadata
   accompanying the data delivery, in accordance with Art. 4(6)
   second sentence.]

3. Confidentiality undertaking

   The User shall:
   (a) treat the trade-secret data as confidential;
   (b) not disclose the trade-secret data to any third party
       except as permitted under this agreement;
   (c) restrict internal sharing of the trade-secret data to
       [named personnel / specified roles / a defined access list];
   (d) use the trade-secret data only for [defined purposes
       consistent with the User's request and Art. 4(10)
       restrictions].

   The User's obligations under this section 3 continue for
   [defined period; typically the duration that the data retains
   trade-secret character; some agreements extend the obligation
   indefinitely with carve-outs for data that becomes generally
   known through no fault of the User].

4. Technical measures

   The parties agree the following technical measures:

   [Calibrated to the sensitivity of the data and the User's
   technical capabilities. Examples:

   (a) Encryption. The trade-secret data is encrypted in transit
       and at rest, using [specified algorithm / minimum strength].
       [Optional: Key management on the Data Holder side, with the
       User receiving access via authenticated session.]

   (b) Access controls. Access to the trade-secret data is
       restricted to authenticated, authorised personnel via
       [single sign-on / role-based access control / multi-factor
       authentication]. The User's access list is maintained at
       [shared register / User's internal register, with copy
       provided to the Data Holder on reasonable request].

   (c) Watermarking and fingerprinting. The trade-secret data
       includes watermarks or fingerprints that allow the Data
       Holder to identify the source of any leak.

   (d) Audit logging. The User maintains immutable logs of access
       and use of the trade-secret data. The Data Holder may, on
       [number] working days' notice, audit the logs for the
       purpose of verifying compliance with this agreement.

   (e) Sandboxed environment. [Where applicable to the use case:]
       The User processes the trade-secret data only within an
       environment [provided by / approved by / specified by] the
       Data Holder, preventing local copies and uncontrolled
       export.

   (f) Data minimisation. The User receives only the trade-secret
       data necessary for the agreed purposes; surrounding non-
       trade-secret data is provided separately.]

5. Organisational measures

   The parties agree the following organisational measures:

   [Calibrated to the User's organisation. Examples:

   (a) Restricted-access team. The User designates [number] named
       individuals authorised to access the trade-secret data.
       Changes to the access list are notified to the Data Holder
       within [number] working days.

   (b) Training. The User ensures that authorised personnel are
       trained on the trade-secret nature of the data and the
       consequences of misuse.

   (c) Sub-recipient controls. The User shall not share the
       trade-secret data within its corporate group or with any
       sub-recipient, except as expressly permitted in writing by
       the Data Holder.

   (d) Incident response. The User shall notify the Data Holder
       of any suspected confidentiality breach within [number]
       hours of discovery and cooperate in good faith in the
       investigation.

   (e) Return or erasure. On termination of the agreed use, the
       User returns or erases the trade-secret data and certifies
       erasure to the Data Holder.]

6. Proportionality

   The parties acknowledge that the measures agreed in sections 4
   and 5 are proportionate within the meaning of Art. 4(6) to the
   trade-secret data covered by this agreement, taking into
   account the sensitivity of the data, the User's intended use,
   the User's technical and organisational capabilities, and the
   exposure to third parties.

   The parties agree to review the measures at intervals of
   [number] months and to adjust them in good faith where
   technical or operational developments require.

7. Use restrictions (cross-reference)

   The User's use of the trade-secret data is also subject to:
   (a) the Art. 4(10) restrictions of the Data Act (no use to
       develop a connected product that competes with the
       connected product from which the data originates; no
       sharing with a third party with that intent; no use to
       derive insights about the economic situation, assets or
       production methods of the manufacturer or the Data
       Holder);
   (b) the underlying Trade Secrets Directive (Directive (EU)
       2016/943) framework, including the lawful and unlawful
       acquisition, use and disclosure provisions of Arts. 3 and
       4 of that Directive;
   (c) any applicable sectoral law.

8. Stage-2 and stage-3 reservation

   The Data Holder reserves the right, in the event that the User
   fails to implement the measures agreed in this agreement or
   undermines the confidentiality of the trade secrets, to
   withhold or suspend data sharing pursuant to Art. 4(7) of the
   Data Act. The Data Holder reserves the right, in exceptional
   circumstances where it is able to demonstrate that disclosure
   despite the measures agreed in this agreement is highly likely
   to cause serious and irreparable economic damage, to refuse on
   a case-by-case basis pursuant to Art. 4(8).

9. Redress and dispute settlement

   In the event of dispute between the parties concerning the
   measures or their implementation, the User retains its rights
   under Art. 4(9), including the right to lodge a complaint with
   the competent authority designated pursuant to Art. 37 and the
   right to agree to refer the matter to a dispute settlement
   body in accordance with Art. 10(1).

10. Governing law and jurisdiction

    [Member State law]. [Court designation, typically the courts
    of the Data Holder's place of establishment; subject to
    consumer protection rules where the User is a consumer.]

[Signature blocks for both parties]
```
