# Template: Art. 6 third-party recipient contract

Produces the contract between the user and the third party recipient that operationalises a user-directed Art. 5(1) data flow, with terms that comply with the closed list of prohibitions in Art. 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act). This is the contract between the user and the third party; the contract between the data holder and the third party (for FRAND terms and compensation under Arts. 8 and 9) is a separate document.

Form: bilateral contract scaffolding. Markdown by default. Length varies with the operational scope; typically three to five pages once filled in.

---

```
Data-use agreement under Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854
(Data Act)

Between:
   [User, full legal entity name / natural person name and
   address] ("the User")

And:
   [Third party, full legal entity name and registered address]
   ("the Third Party")

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to the user's Art. 5(1)
            instruction to the data holder dated [date]]

---

1. Purpose and scope

   The User has, by Art. 5(1) request to [data holder] dated
   [date], directed the data holder to make available to the
   Third Party readily available data from [connected product /
   related service identifiers]. This agreement governs the
   Third Party's processing of that data and the parties'
   respective obligations under Art. 6 of the Data Act.

   The User confirms that the Third Party is not an undertaking
   designated as a gatekeeper pursuant to Art. 3 of Regulation
   (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act). The Third Party
   warrants the same as at the date of this agreement, with a
   continuing obligation to inform the User without undue delay
   in the event of designation.

2. Data covered

   The data covered by this agreement is:

   [Specific identification of the categories of data the Third
   Party will receive from the data holder. Should mirror the
   User's Art. 5(1) request.]

3. Permitted purposes

   The Third Party shall process the data covered by this
   agreement only for the following purposes:

   [Specific purposes, expressed with sufficient precision to
   support enforcement. Examples: "providing a predictive-
   maintenance service to the User in relation to [identified
   connected product]"; "providing fleet-management analytics to
   the User"; "operating the [named service] that the User has
   subscribed to."]

   Use for any other purpose requires the User's prior written
   agreement.

4. Closed list of prohibitions (Art. 6(2))

   The Third Party shall not:

   (a) make the exercise of choices or rights under Art. 5 of
       the Data Act and Art. 6 of the Data Act by the User
       unduly difficult, including by offering choices to the
       User in a non-neutral manner, or by coercing, deceiving
       or manipulating the User, or by subverting or impairing
       the autonomy, decision-making or choices of the User,
       including by means of a user digital interface or a part
       thereof;

   (b) notwithstanding Art. 22(2), points (a) and (c), of
       Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), use the data it receives
       for profiling, unless it is necessary to provide the
       service requested by the User;

   (c) make the data it receives available to another third
       party, unless the data is made available on the basis of
       a contract with the User and provided that the other
       third party takes all necessary measures agreed between
       the data holder and the Third Party to preserve the
       confidentiality of trade secrets;

   (d) make the data it receives available to an undertaking
       designated as a gatekeeper pursuant to Art. 3 of
       Regulation (EU) 2022/1925;

   (e) use the data it receives to develop a product that
       competes with the connected product from which the
       accessed data originate or share the data with another
       third party for that purpose; the Third Party shall also
       not use any non-personal product data or related service
       data made available to it to derive insights about the
       economic situation, assets and production methods of, or
       use by, the data holder;

   (f) use the data it receives in a manner that has an adverse
       impact on the security of the connected product or
       related service;

   (g) disregard the specific measures agreed with the data
       holder or with the trade secrets holder pursuant to Art.
       5(9) of the Data Act and undermine the confidentiality of
       trade secrets;

   (h) [where the User is a consumer:] prevent the User, on the
       basis of this agreement or otherwise, from making the
       data it receives available to other parties.

   These prohibitions are independent and cumulative. Breach of
   any prohibition is breach of this agreement and may also
   constitute a breach of the Data Act enforceable by the
   competent authority designated pursuant to Art. 37.

5. Trade-secret safeguards under Art. 5(9)

   The Third Party shall comply with the technical and
   organisational measures agreed with the data holder under
   Art. 5(9) of the Data Act in relation to any trade-secret-
   protected data within the scope of this agreement. The Third
   Party warrants that it has the technical and organisational
   capability to implement the measures agreed with the data
   holder and that it has authorised personnel trained on the
   trade-secret nature of the data.

   The Third Party shall notify the User of any suspected
   confidentiality breach affecting the trade-secret-protected
   data within [number] hours of discovery, and cooperate with
   the User and the data holder in the investigation.

6. Data minimisation and erasure

   The Third Party shall:
   (a) access and process only the data necessary for the
       purposes set out in section 3;
   (b) erase the data when no longer necessary for the agreed
       purpose, unless the User has separately agreed, in
       writing, an extended retention period in relation to
       non-personal data;
   (c) certify erasure to the User on the User's reasonable
       request.

7. Personal data and the GDPR

   [Where the data covered includes personal data:]

   To the extent that the data covered includes personal data:

   (a) The roles of the parties are as follows: [identify
   controllerships. Common patterns: User is controller, Third
   Party is processor under Art. 28 GDPR; User and Third Party
   are joint controllers under Art. 26 GDPR; Third Party is an
   independent controller of personal data it processes for its
   own purposes]. The GDPR roles are documented separately in
   [Annex / DPA / joint-controller arrangement].

   (b) The Third Party shall comply with the GDPR and any
   applicable national law on the protection of personal data.

   (c) Where the Third Party is a processor, the parties enter
   into the data processing agreement at [Annex / separate
   document]. Where the Third Party is a controller for its own
   purposes, the Third Party shall have a valid Art. 6(1) GDPR
   legal basis and shall inform data subjects in accordance with
   Arts. 13 and 14 GDPR.

   (d) Where the User is not the data subject, the User confirms
   it has informed the Third Party of the legal basis on which
   the data was made available to the Third Party by the data
   holder under Art. 5(7) of the Data Act.

   (e) Nothing in this agreement adversely affects the rights of
   data subjects pursuant to Art. 5(13) of the Data Act and
   applicable law.

8. Liability and remedies

   Each party is liable for breach of this agreement in
   accordance with [governing law]. The Third Party indemnifies
   the User against [calibrated indemnity scope, e.g. losses
   arising from the Third Party's breach of section 4 (closed
   list of prohibitions) or section 5 (trade-secret
   safeguards)].

   Neither party excludes or limits liability for intentional
   acts or gross negligence. Any term purporting to do so is
   void and severable in accordance with Art. 13(7) of the Data
   Act and applicable contract law.

9. Term and termination

   This agreement is in force from [start date] for a term of
   [period or "the duration of the User's Art. 5(1)
   instruction"]. Either party may terminate on [reasonable
   notice period]. Termination does not relieve the Third Party
   of the post-termination erasure obligation in section 6, the
   confidentiality obligations in section 5, or the GDPR
   obligations in section 7.

10. Dispute resolution

    Disputes are resolved in accordance with [governing law].
    The parties may also refer disputes to a dispute settlement
    body where eligible under Art. 10(1) of the Data Act. The
    User retains the right to lodge a complaint with the
    competent authority designated pursuant to Art. 37.

11. Variation and severability

    Variations are in writing signed by both parties. Where any
    term is unenforceable, the remaining terms continue in
    force. Art. 13(7) of the Data Act applies to any
    unilaterally imposed term found to be unfair.

[Signature blocks for both parties]

Annexes:
- [Where applicable: data processing agreement under GDPR Art. 28]
- [Where applicable: joint-controller arrangement under GDPR Art.
  26]
- [Where applicable: list of authorised personnel; technical
  security specification; the trade-secret safeguards agreement
  between the data holder and the Third Party]
```
