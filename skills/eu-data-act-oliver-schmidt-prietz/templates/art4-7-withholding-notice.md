# Template: Art. 4(7) withholding or suspension notice (stage 2)

Produces the data holder's stage-2 written, duly substantiated decision to withhold or suspend the sharing of data identified as trade secrets where one of the three Art. 4(7) triggers is met, with the parallel competent authority notification.

Form: dual-output. (i) A notice to the user, and (ii) a competent authority notification. Both required. Markdown by default.

Use this template only after stage-1 negotiation under Art. 4(6) has occurred and one of the three Art. 4(7) triggers is met:
- No agreement on the necessary measures despite good-faith negotiation;
- The user has agreed to measures but failed to implement them; or
- The user has undermined the confidentiality of the trade secrets.

Do NOT use this template as a substitute for stage 1. If stage-1 negotiation has not yet been attempted, route to `art4-6-trade-secret-safeguards-agreement.md`. Do NOT use it for outright refusal of an Art. 4(1) request; that is stage 3 under `art4-8-refusal-letter.md`.

---

## Part 1: Withholding/suspension notice to the user

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [User, full legal entity name / natural person name and
     address]

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference; reference to the underlying Art.
            4(1) request and to any Art. 4(6) safeguards
            agreement]

Subject: Decision to [withhold / suspend] sharing of trade-
         secret-protected data pursuant to Article 4(7) of
         Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Background

   The User submitted a request for access to data under Art.
   4(1) of the Data Act dated [date]. The Data Holder identified
   certain data within the scope of the request as trade-secret-
   protected within the meaning of Art. 2(19) Data Act and Art.
   2(1) of Directive (EU) 2016/943 (Trade Secrets Directive). The
   parties [agreed safeguards on [date] / engaged in stage-1
   negotiation under Art. 4(6) from [date]].

2. The trigger for this notice

   [Identify which of the three Art. 4(7) triggers is engaged.
   Use only the trigger that the facts support; do not combine
   triggers unless multiple are genuinely independently engaged.]

   [Trigger 1: No agreement on the necessary measures]

   The parties have engaged in good-faith negotiation under Art.
   4(6) since [date]. The negotiation has not resulted in
   agreement on the necessary technical and organisational
   measures, for the following reasons:

   [Factual account of the negotiation. Identify the measures the
   Data Holder proposed, the measures the User accepted, and the
   measures on which agreement could not be reached. Identify the
   reasons the User has stated for not agreeing to the Data
   Holder's proposed measures, and the reasons the Data Holder
   considers the User's counter-proposals insufficient.]

   The Data Holder has, in good faith, sought proportionate
   measures. The continued absence of agreement on [specific
   measures] prevents the Data Holder from disclosing the trade-
   secret-protected data with the required preservation of
   confidentiality.

   [Trigger 2: Failure to implement agreed measures]

   The parties agreed safeguards under Art. 4(6) by agreement
   dated [date]. The User has failed to implement the following
   agreed measures:

   [Specific identification of which agreed measures the User has
   not implemented. Factual account of the implementation
   failure: what the User was required to do, what the User has
   done, when the Data Holder discovered the failure, and any
   prior notice the Data Holder gave the User.]

   The Data Holder has [identified the failure on [date] / given
   the User notice on [date] to remedy the failure, which has
   not been remedied within [period]].

   [Trigger 3: Undermining the confidentiality of the trade
   secrets]

   The User has undermined the confidentiality of the trade
   secrets as follows:

   [Specific factual account of the undermining: what action the
   User took, when, the effect on the trade-secret confidentiality,
   and any consequences for the Data Holder. The undermining must
   be demonstrated; mere suspicion is insufficient.]

3. Identification of the trade secrets affected

   The trade-secret data the sharing of which is [withheld /
   suspended] by this notice is:

   [Category-by-category or field-by-field identification. Where
   any of the trade secrets have had their confidentiality
   undermined under Trigger 3, identify which.]

4. The decision

   On the basis set out in sections 2 and 3, the Data Holder
   [withholds / suspends] the sharing of the trade-secret-
   protected data identified in section 3, in accordance with
   Art. 4(7) of the Data Act.

   [Where withholding (data not yet shared): "The Data Holder
   will not share the affected data unless and until [specific
   remediation that would enable sharing to proceed; e.g. the
   User agrees to the necessary measures, implements the
   previously agreed measures, or remedies the undermining]."]

   [Where suspending (data partly already shared): "The Data
   Holder suspends the sharing of any further data in the
   affected categories. Data already shared continues to be
   subject to the existing safeguards and to the User's
   obligations under Art. 4(10), Art. 4(11), and the Trade
   Secrets Directive."]

5. Competent authority notification

   The Data Holder has, in parallel with this notice, notified
   the competent authority of [Member State of establishment]
   designated pursuant to Art. 37 of the decision and of the
   measures that have not been agreed or implemented and, where
   relevant, the trade secrets that have had their confidentiality
   undermined. A copy of the notification is enclosed.

6. The User's redress options

   The User retains, without prejudice to the right to seek
   redress at any stage before a court or tribunal of a Member
   State:
   (a) the right to lodge a complaint with the competent
       authority designated pursuant to Art. 37 in accordance
       with Art. 37(5)(b). The competent authority shall,
       without undue delay, decide whether and under which
       conditions data sharing is to start or resume; and
   (b) the right to agree with the Data Holder to refer the
       matter to a dispute settlement body in accordance with
       Art. 10(1).

7. Remediation pathway (where appropriate)

   [Where withholding under Trigger 1: "The Data Holder remains
   open to continued negotiation on proportionate measures. If
   the User wishes to propose adjustments, the Data Holder will
   engage in good faith."]

   [Where withholding under Trigger 2: "The Data Holder will
   resume sharing upon the User's implementation of the agreed
   measures and the User's confirmation, supported by [evidence
   the User must provide], of implementation."]

   [Where suspending under Trigger 3: "The Data Holder will
   resume sharing only upon [specific remediation: e.g.
   restoration of the compromised technical measure, removal of
   data from unauthorised recipients, supplementary safeguards
   demonstrably effective against further undermining]."]

[Signature block placeholder]

Enclosures:
- Copy of the competent authority notification dated [date]
- Where applicable: the Art. 4(6) safeguards agreement; the
  negotiation correspondence; evidence of failure to implement
  or of undermining.
```

## Part 2: Competent authority notification

```
[Data holder letterhead placeholder]

To: [Competent authority designated pursuant to Art. 37 of the
     Data Act in the Member State of [establishment]]

Date: [Date]

Reference: [Internal reference]

Subject: Notification of withholding or suspension of data
         sharing pursuant to Article 4(7) of Regulation (EU)
         2023/2854 (Data Act)

1. Notifying entity

   [Data holder, full legal entity name, registered address,
   contact point under Art. 37 if any.]

2. Subject matter

   The Data Holder has, by notice to the User dated [date],
   decided to [withhold / suspend] the sharing of trade-secret-
   protected data identified within the scope of the User's Art.
   4(1) request dated [date]. A copy of the notice to the User
   is enclosed.

3. The Art. 4(7) trigger and identification

   In accordance with the third sentence of Art. 4(7), the Data
   Holder notifies the competent authority of:

   (a) which measures have not been agreed or implemented:
       [specific identification];
   (b) where relevant, which trade secrets have had their
       confidentiality undermined: [specific identification].

4. User information

   The User against whom the [withholding / suspension] takes
   effect is [name, contact details if appropriate]. The User has
   been informed of its redress options under Art. 4(9) and Art.
   37(5)(b).

5. Stage-1 record (supporting documentation)

   The Data Holder encloses, in support of this notification:
   - The Art. 4(6) safeguards agreement [where Trigger 2 or 3];
   - The negotiation correspondence [where Trigger 1];
   - Evidence of failure to implement or of undermining [where
     Trigger 2 or 3].

[Signature block placeholder]
```
