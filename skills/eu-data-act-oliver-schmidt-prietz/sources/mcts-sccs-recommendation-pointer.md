## Scope: what is in the package

### Model Contractual Terms (MCTs) for data access and use

Four sets of MCTs cover the relationships in which data sharing under Chapters II and III of the Data Act is relevant:

1. **Data Holder to User MCT.** For the contract between a data holder and a user under Chapter II. Addresses the right of access, the modalities for direct or indirect access, transparency obligations under Article 3, use of non-personal data by the data holder under Article 4(13), trade-secret safeguards, and user identification.
2. **User to Data Recipient MCT.** For the contract between a user and a data recipient designated by the user under Article 5. Addresses the third-party's use limitations under Article 6(2), trade-secret safeguards inherited from the data holder, and gatekeeper exclusion under Article 5(3).
3. **Data Holder to Data Recipient MCT.** For mandatory data sharing under Chapter III. Addresses fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms, compensation under Article 9, and trade-secret protection under Article 8(6).
4. **Data Sharer to Data Recipient MCT.** For voluntary data-sharing arrangements outside the mandatory regime, drafted to be compliant with Chapter IV's unfairness control.

All four sets are designed to be compliant with the rules of Chapter IV on unfair contract terms.

### Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for cloud computing contracts

Three sets of SCCs translate the provisions of Chapter VI into ready-to-use contractual terms:

1. **SCC Switching & Exit.** Covers the customer's right to switch, the notice period (max 2 months under Article 25(2)(d)), the transition period (max 30 calendar days under Article 25(2)(a), extendable to 7 months under Article 25(4) in cases of technical unfeasibility), the retrieval period (min 30 calendar days under Article 25(2)(g)), and erasure obligations.
2. **SCC Term & Termination.** Covers the duration of the contract, termination rights, and the interaction between termination and switching.
3. **SCC Security & Business Continuity.** Covers security during switching, provider notification of significant incidents, and continuity of service obligations under Article 25(2)(a)(iv).

The SCCs also cover non-dispersion, non-amendment, and liability elements that go beyond Chapter VI.

## How the skill should use this Recommendation

- **Drafting benchmark, not safe harbour.** When advising a client on Chapter II, Chapter III, or Chapter VI contracts, the skill should treat the MCTs/SCCs as a high-quality starting point that reflects the Commission's view on what fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory terms look like. The skill should not claim that adopting them verbatim discharges any specific Data Act obligation. The unfairness control under Article 13 still applies to every term in scope, regardless of whether it tracks the MCT.
- **Cross-reference, do not redistribute.** Outputs that recommend MCT/SCC language should cite the Commission's URL and the specific MCT or SCC set by name. They should not paste clause text from this skill into deliverables; clause text may change after the Recommendation is updated, and the authoritative version is the one on the Commission's page.
- **Watch for the Digital Omnibus.** The Commission's proposed amendments to the Data Act (COM(2025) 833 final, 19 November 2025) include changes that affect Chapter VI (in particular Article 31 carve-outs for custom-built services, SME and small-mid-cap exemptions, and Article 25 early-termination penalties). If the proposal is adopted, the SCCs may be revised. The skill should flag this in any Chapter VI deliverable that references the SCCs as drafting input.
- **Helpdesk integration.** For client questions that fall outside the FAQ and outside the MCTs/SCCs, the Commission has launched the Data Act Legal Helpdesk at https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act-legal-helpdesk. The skill can suggest this as a follow-up channel for non-binding Commission views on edge cases.

## What this file is not

- Not a copy of the clause text. The Commission's own page is the source of truth.
- Not a determination of whether any specific term is fair under Article 13. That assessment is matter-by-matter and depends on the bargaining context, the imposing party, and the broader contract.
- Not a substitute for the regulation. The MCTs/SCCs are interpretive aids; the operative norms are in Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 itself.
