# AI Omnibus 2026 — Timeline postponements

## Original Art. 113 timeline (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as adopted June 2024)

| Provision | Original effective date |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Art. 6(2) (Annex III high-risk obligations) | 2 August 2026 |
| Art. 6(1) (Annex I high-risk obligations) | 2 August 2027 |
| Art. 111(2) legacy cut-off (systems placed on market before date X) | 2 August 2026 |

## Postponed dates under the AI Omnibus 2026

The AI Omnibus (legislative package proposed by the Commission and progressing through co-legislators) postpones these dates:

| Provision | Original date | **Omnibus date** |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| Art. 6(2) (Annex III) | 2 August 2026 | **2 December 2027** |
| Art. 6(1) (Annex I) | 2 August 2027 | **2 August 2028** |
| Art. 111(2) legacy cut-off | 2 August 2026 | **2 December 2027** |

These dates are now confirmed in the Commission's Draft Guidelines on Art. 6 classification (general principles, ¶448).

## Art. 50 transparency — NOT postponed by the Omnibus

Art. 50 (Chapter IV transparency) is **not** a high-risk obligation and is **not** moved by the AI Omnibus. It is governed by the Art. 113 **general application date of 2 August 2026**. Two common errors: filing Art. 50 under the postponed Annex III date (2 Dec 2027), or under the 2 Aug 2025 GPAI/governance tranche (Art. 113(b)). Both are wrong — Art. 50 is neither high-risk (so not postponed) nor in the 113(b) tranche.

| Art. 50 obligation | Application date |
|--------------------|------------------|
| 50(1) AI-interaction disclosure | 2 August 2026 (no transition) |
| 50(3) emotion-recognition / biometric-categorisation notice | 2 August 2026 (no transition) |
| 50(4) deepfake & AI-generated-text disclosure | 2 August 2026 (no transition) |
| 50(2) machine-readable marking — systems placed on the market **on/after** 2 Aug 2026 | 2 August 2026 |
| 50(2) machine-readable marking — **legacy** systems placed on the market **before** 2 Aug 2026 | **2 December 2026** *(Omnibus 4-month grace)* |

The **only** Omnibus relief touching Art. 50 is the four-month grace for *legacy* generative-AI marking under 50(2): systems on the market before 2 Aug 2026 get until **2 December 2026** (the Commission had originally proposed 2 Feb 2027). All other Art. 50 duties apply from 2 Aug 2026 with no transition, and systems newly placed on the market on/after 2 Aug 2026 must comply from day one.

## Implications

### For providers and deployers

- Annex III high-risk obligations apply ~16 months later than originally planned.
- The legacy cut-off extension means more systems already in service before the new date benefit from the Art. 111(2) "no significant change" carve-out.
- Public-authority deployers must still comply by 2 August 2030 regardless (Art. 111(2) second sentence).
- Large-scale IT systems (Annex X) must comply by 31 December 2030 (Art. 111(1)).

### For this skill

- All effective-date references in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) and downstream artefacts use the Omnibus dates.
- The Commission guidelines themselves are dated 2026 (post-Omnibus); they cite the postponed dates.

## Status caveat

The AI Omnibus is provisionally agreed but **not yet in force**: Council general approach 13 Mar 2026, provisional inter-institutional agreement 7 May 2026, European Parliament plenary adoption June 2026 (P10_TA(2026)0198) — **pending Council formal adoption and Official Journal publication** (expected before 2 Aug 2026). Treat these as the operative planning dates, not yet black-letter law. **Last verified: June 2026.** This file is the single source of truth for AI Act effective dates across the suite; downstream files should cite it rather than hard-coding dates separately.
