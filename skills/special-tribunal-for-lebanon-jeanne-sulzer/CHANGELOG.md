### Skill scope at v1.0.0
- Covers the entire STL lifecycle (establishment 2007, operations 2009-2023, closure 31 December 2023) plus residual matters
- Encodes the same verification-first methodology as the other skills in this repository
- Specifically equips the user to handle the **2011 Interlocutory Decision** with appropriate care for its contested status
- Maps the Appeals Chamber convictions of Merhi and Oneissi (10 March 2022, sentenced 16 June 2022) — frequently missing from summaries that report only the 18 August 2020 Trial Judgment

### Known limitations
- The STL produced an enormous procedural record (hundreds of decisions). This skill maps the principal decisions; specific procedural orders on disclosure, witness protection, in-court evidentiary rulings, and similar interlocutory matters are left for case-by-case verification at runtime.
- Connected attack cases (STL-18-10) have their own evolving procedural status — verify current disposition against stl-tsl.org.
- The skill flags but does not resolve the scholarly debate on the customary international law status of transnational terrorism. The skill enables accurate citation of the STL's finding; the doctrinal debate is left to the user.
