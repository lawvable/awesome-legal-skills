# Model Privacy Policy — Drafting Notes

The Model Privacy Policy ("Model Policy") is a 7-page draft prepared by S&R Associates (as reflected in comments) for a media/broadcasting company referred to as "ABC." It serves as a reference template for consent notice structure and language under DPDPA.

## Key Structural Features to Replicate

1. **Notice is standalone**: Scope section clearly states the notice is given for ABC's specific services, and cross-references other policies (Terms of Use, Privacy Policy) without making the notice depend on them.

2. **Services are defined**: Section 2 defines "ABC Services" with specificity — not just a generic "our products."

3. **Personal data is itemised**: Section 3 gives 9 sub-categories, each with specific examples. Mimic this enumerated format.

4. **Purposes are numbered**: Section 4 uses 15 numbered sub-purposes. Each links to a recognisable function. Replicate this numbered list format.

5. **Third-party sharing is categorised**: Section 5 uses 4 categories of recipients (vendors/service providers; affiliates/partners; investors/acquirers; law enforcement). Note that specific data shared with each category is implied by context — the new notice should be more explicit about which data goes to which recipient category.

6. **Children's data is handled bluntly**: Section 6 uses all-caps to state the service is NOT intended for under-18s. This is a valid and clean approach for services that exclude children. If the service does serve children, this approach cannot be used.

7. **Rights section is comprehensive**: Section 9 covers all 7 DPDPA rights in order, with plain-language explanations. The Model Policy does not include actionable instructions per right — **our notice should improve on this** by adding specific contact details, portal links, and timelines for each right.

8. **Retention uses DPDPA language**: Section 10 mirrors the statutory language of S.8(7)/(8). Use similar language but also reference the specific time periods from Rule 8 and Third Schedule.

9. **Grievance section has a structured format**: Section 13 uses a name/email/address block for the Grievance Officer — replicate this.

10. **Consent box is at the end**: Sections 14 and the sign-off box follow the substance. The checkbox language distinguishes between adult users and legal guardians.

## Improvements Our Notice Should Make on the Model Policy

| Area | Model Policy Gap | Our Notice Should Add |
|------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Language option | S&R notes it will be built into the interface — not in draft | Flag the 22 Eighth Schedule languages explicitly in the notice body |
| Rights - actionability | Rights stated but no "how to exercise" instructions | Add specific email, portal link, timeline for each right |
| Data categories | Good but written for a specific broadcaster | Tailor to user's actual industry |
| Retention periods | General statement | Add specific Rule 8 / Third Schedule periods for applicable sector |
| Security safeguards | Very brief (Section 11) | Add specifics per Rule 6 requirements |
| Cookies | Section 8 is broad and not categorised by type | Categorise by type (essential, functional, analytics, advertising) |
| International transfers | One sentence in Section 11 | Add specifics: which countries, which safeguards, user consent |
| Automated decision-making | Not addressed | Add if applicable |
| Consent Manager | Not mentioned | Note that consent can be managed via a Consent Manager if applicable |
| Legal mapping table | Not present | Include at end of notice |

## Tone Reference

The Model Policy strikes a reasonable balance — professional but accessible. Key phrases worth adapting:
- "We are committed to protecting your personal data and processing it lawfully."
- "By accepting the terms of this Notice, you hereby consent to the processing of your Personal Information in the manner and for the Purposes indicated in this notice and the Privacy Policy."
- "You can decide not to provide or withdraw your consent... however if you do so, [Company] may not be able to provide you with the requested products/services."
