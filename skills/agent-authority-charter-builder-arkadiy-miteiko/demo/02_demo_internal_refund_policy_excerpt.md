## Policy Purpose

This policy defines when customer refund and fee-waiver requests may be approved, escalated, or declined.

The policy is intended to support consistent handling of low-value refund and fee-waiver requests while preserving human review for complex, sensitive, disputed, or high-risk cases.

## Eligible Low-Value Refunds

A refund or fee waiver may be approved when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. the amount requested is USD 50 or less;
2. the customer account is in good standing;
3. the charge occurred within the prior 90 days;
4. the customer has not received more than one courtesy refund in the prior 180 days;
5. there is no open fraud flag;
6. there is no open dispute or chargeback;
7. there is no active legal, regulatory, or formal complaint;
8. the support ticket contains enough information to identify the relevant charge;
9. the policy basis for approval is clear;
10. approval would not conflict with another internal restriction.

## Cases Requiring Human Review

Human review is required for:

- refunds or fee waivers above USD 50;
- repeated refund requests;
- customers with open disputes;
- customers with fraud flags;
- complaints using legal or regulatory language;
- complaints involving hardship, vulnerability, illness, bereavement, disability, unemployment, or similar sensitivity;
- requests involving possible discrimination or unfair treatment;
- cases involving ambiguous or incomplete transaction data;
- cases where policy interpretation is uncertain;
- cases where a previous human reviewer denied the same request;
- cases that may create reputational or regulatory risk.

## Prohibited Automated Decisions

Automated systems may not:

- deny refund or fee-waiver requests without human review;
- send external communications to customers;
- approve exceptions outside policy;
- approve refunds above the approved threshold;
- override prior human decisions;
- delete or alter case records;
- modify audit logs;
- make decisions based on protected characteristics;
- continue processing if system access, policy data, or evidence logging fails.

## Internal Notes

If a refund or fee waiver is approved, the case file must include:

- customer request summary;
- amount requested;
- transaction identifier;
- policy condition satisfied;
- approval basis;
- reviewer or system identifier;
- timestamp;
- evidence of account standing;
- evidence that no fraud flag or open dispute was present.

## Escalation Path

Routine escalations: Customer Operations Reviewer  
Potential fraud: Fraud Operations  
Legal or regulatory complaint: Legal and Compliance  
Vulnerable customer or hardship: Customer Care Specialist  
Policy ambiguity: Customer Compliance Manager  
System or audit failure: AI Engineering Lead and Operational Risk Lead
