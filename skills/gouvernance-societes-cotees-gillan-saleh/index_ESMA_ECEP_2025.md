# Index analytique de la doctrine — skill gouvernance-emetteurs-cotes

## Document indexé : ESMA — European Common Enforcement Priorities for 2025 Corporate Reporting

**Référence complète** : European Securities and Markets Authority, *European Common Enforcement Priorities for 2025 Corporate Reporting*, Statement ESMA32-2064178921-9254, 14 octobre 2025, 9 pages.

**Langue du document** : anglais.

**Nature du document** : déclaration annuelle de l'ESMA fixant les **priorités communes de supervision européenne** (ECEP) pour les rapports financiers annuels 2025 des émetteurs admis à la négociation sur les marchés réglementés de l'EEE.

**Cadre juridique** : article 4 of the Transparency Directive (2004/109/EC), incluant les états de durabilité et les exigences ESEF.

**Périmètre temporel** : rapports financiers annuels **2025** (publication par les émetteurs en 2026).

**Vérification pagination** : pagination physique = pagination logique confirmée. Document de 9 pages.

**Structure** : 4 sections.
- Section 1 (p. 2-4) : Priorités IFRS — 2 priorités
- Section 2 (p. 5-7) : Priorités états de durabilité — 2 priorités
- Section 3 (p. 8) : Priorité ESEF — 1 priorité
- Section 4 (p. 9) : Considérations générales et rappels (hors ECEP)

**Approche** : *« ESMA and the European national enforcers will pay particular attention to these areas when examining the application of the reporting requirements. »* Sur la base des examens conduits, *« enforcement actions will be taken whenever material misstatements are identified. ESMA will then report on its findings. »*

**Responsabilités rappelées** : p. 1. *« ESMA highlights the responsibility of issuers' management and supervisory bodies, as well as the importance of the supervisory role of audit committees »* pour :
- *« Ensuring the overall internal consistency of the annual financial report. »*
- *« Implementing and overseeing internal controls. »*
- *« Contributing to the high quality of annual financial reports. »*

## Cartographie thématique par bloc de la grille

### Bloc D — Comités du conseil

#### Responsabilité du comité d'audit dans l'oversight global du reporting

- **Position ESMA** : p. 1. Rôle de supervision des comités d'audit rappelé pour assurer la cohérence interne globale, mettre en œuvre et superviser les contrôles internes, contribuer à la haute qualité des rapports financiers annuels.

### Bloc H — Comply or explain (supervision)

#### Méthodologie de supervision européenne

- **Cadre** : p. 1. *« ESMA and the European national enforcers will take enforcement actions whenever material misstatements are identified. »*
- **Priorités nationales additionnelles** : p. 1. *« In addition to these European priorities, enforcers may also set national priorities. »*
- **Application proportionnée** : p. 1. *« These recommendations should be considered in the light of their materiality and relevance for the issuer's operations and its annual financial report. »*

### Bloc J — Information sur la durabilité (CSRD) — *avec sous-rubriques J.1 à J.7*

#### J.1 — Cadre normatif (CSRD, ESRS, taxonomie, vérification)

##### Contexte CSRD 2025 — Omnibus et incertitude réglementaire

- **Cartographie de section** : Sustainability reporting, p. 5-7.
- **2025 = première année de reporting CSRD** : p. 5. Pour les grandes entités d'intérêt public dites « Wave 1 ».
- **Omnibus Package (février 2025)** : p. 5. Publié par la Commission européenne pour réduire la charge administrative et accroître la compétitivité.
- **Composantes du paquet Omnibus** : p. 5.
  - **« Content » Directive** : *« proposed amendments to the CSRD — significant reduction in the scope of companies subject to reporting, to the Audit Directive, to the Accounting Directive and to the CSDD Directive. Discussion ongoing by the co-legislators. »*
  - **« Stop the clock » Directive** (adoptée en avril 2025) : *« postpones all reporting requirements for companies in the original CSRD scope that were not required to report in 2025. This postponement includes "Wave 2" companies. »*
  - **Engagement Commission européenne** : *« streamline the first set of ESRS. »*
- **EFRAG exposure draft** : p. 5. **31 juillet 2025** — publication d'un projet de révision des ESRS, sur mandat de la Commission européenne du 27 mars 2025.

##### Position ESMA — applicabilité aux entreprises « Wave 1 » et « Wave 2 »

- **Position ESMA — Wave 1** : p. 5. *« The revised ESRS will only be applicable after publication of the corresponding Delegated Act in the Official Journal. "Wave 1" companies should therefore not rely on the EFRAG exposure draft or on the final technical advice to the Commission to prepare their disclosures for the 2025 financial year. »*
- **« Quick Fix » Delegated Act** : p. 5. *« The European Commission has adopted this Delegated Act to extend the use of several transitional provisions (phase-in). »* Acte sous scrutiny par les co-législateurs. *« ESMA encourages "Wave 1" companies to follow its finalisation to determine their reporting obligations in 2026 for the 2025 financial year. »*
- **Position ESMA — Wave 2** : p. 5. *« "Wave 2" companies are encouraged to follow the transposition of the "Stop the clock" Directive in their Member States to determine their reporting obligations in 2026. »*

##### Documents ESMA antérieurs rappelés

- **Juillet 2024 — Off to a good start** : p. 5. Recommandations sur la mise en œuvre du cadre actuel de reporting de durabilité, *« which remain largely valid and point to the supports published by the European Commission and EFRAG. »*
- **Juin 2025 — Statement « Navigating change together »** : p. 5. *« Statement acknowledging the uncertainty caused by the Omnibus proposals and the uneven transposition of the CSRD, and clarifying the enforcers' approach to enforcement of sustainability reporting in this context. »*

##### Justification du carry-over des priorités

- **Position ESMA** : p. 5. *« Due to the uncertainty linked to the current regulatory context, ESMA has exceptionally carried over two of the priorities from its 2024 ECEP — namely the implementation of ESRS requirements on materiality and the scope and structure of the sustainability statement. »*
- **Motivation** : p. 5. *« These two areas relate to fundamental characteristics of sustainability reporting; through these priorities, ESMA seeks to encourage issuers' progression on their learning curve while acknowledging that certain detailed requirements may be subject to amendment in the revision of the ESRS. »*
- **Fact-finding ESMA** : p. 5, note 42. *« This year's priorities on materiality have also been informed by the results of a fact-finding exercise ("Materiality matters!") on 2024 corporate reporting practices under ESRS Set 1. »*

##### Taxonomy — Position 2025

- **Omnibus Taxonomy** : p. 5. Le paquet Omnibus inclut un Delegated Act amendant le Taxonomy Disclosures Delegated Act et le Climate and Environmental Delegated Act. Adopté par la Commission européenne en **juillet 2025**.
- **Calendrier** : p. 5. *« Will enter into force after the scrutiny period if the co-legislators do not raise objections. Companies are encouraged to apply the revised rules to their 2026 disclosures (2025 financial year) but have the option of applying the previous rules for this cycle. »*
- **Position ESMA** : p. 5. *« Recognising the change in the regulatory environment, ESMA has not included specific recommendations on taxonomy disclosures in the 2025 ECEP. »*

#### J.2 — Analyse de matérialité (impact / financière / double matérialité)

##### Priorité 1 ESMA pour 2025 — Considérations de matérialité dans le reporting ESRS (p. 6-7)

###### Importance des considérations de matérialité

- **Position ESMA** : p. 6. *« Materiality considerations play a key role in sustainability reporting under the ESRS as the results of the two-step assessment (materiality of Impacts, Risks and Opportunities - IROs and materiality of information) determine the thematic disclosures to be provided by the company. »*
- **Double matérialité comme filtre** : p. 6. *« Double materiality is the filter that ensures the decision-usefulness of the reported information for all users of the sustainability statement. »*
- **Soin particulier attendu** : p. 6. *« In light of this structural importance of materiality, ESMA emphasises the particular care that should be applied to the disclosures in ESRS 2 relating to the assessment process followed by the issuer. »*

###### Constat ESMA sur les IRO-1

- **Constat sur les disclosures actuelles** : p. 6. *« Although the ESMA fact-finding has shown that this has led to detailed accounts of the methodology and procedural steps, the resulting disclosures were in some cases boilerplate when they mainly reproduced the concepts defined in ESRS 1 or the generic approach suggested in the EFRAG Implementation Guidance on Materiality Assessment (IG1). »*
- **Insuffisance** : p. 6. *« Insufficient insight was sometimes provided into how individual issuers had tailored these criteria and steps to their own facts and circumstances. »*

###### Processus d'évaluation de matérialité — input parameters

- **Datapoint clé** : p. 6. **Datapoint on the input parameters of the materiality assessment process**. *« Aims to provide insight into the basis for determining whether an IRO is material. »*
- **Inputs attendus** : p. 6.
  - *« Data sources. »*
  - *« Scope of operations covered (including in relation to specific geographies or activities). »*
  - *« Other considerations (key assumptions made). »*

###### Materiality thresholds

- **Disclosures attendues sur les seuils** : p. 6. *« Should help users of the sustainability statement understand the principal considerations underpinning the determination of material matters, especially for matters where issuers were most uncertain about materiality during the process. »*
- **Pour les impacts négatifs** : p. 6. *« To be detailed via the severity scale applied for particular matters. »*
- **Pour les risques** : p. 6. *« Disclosure of the indicator used to set the threshold, or even of the quantitative threshold itself may be most insightful. »*

###### Impacts bruts

- **Position ESMA** : p. 6. *« ESMA expects issuers to be transparent on how they have considered gross impacts (i.e. before the effect of any prevention, mitigation or remediation action) in their materiality assessment process. »*

###### Parties prenantes affectées

- **Position ESMA** : p. 6. *« The information concerning engagement with internal and external stakeholders for materiality assessment refers to affected stakeholders. This group of stakeholders should be identifiable, and the associated disclosures should help users understand how their interests and views have been integrated in the materiality process, where applicable. »*

###### Résultats de l'évaluation de matérialité — SBM-3 and IRO-2

- **Importance des disclosures SBM-3 et IRO-2** : p. 6-7. *« ESMA emphasises the importance of the ESRS 2 disclosures (SBM-3 and IRO-2) that can serve as entry points to the sustainability statement for users. »*
- **Vision complète attendue** : p. 6. *« These disclosures should provide a complete view of the issuer's material IROs and their relationship with its strategy and business model, and also guide the user on where and how the management of these IROs (including entity-specific IROs) is addressed in the thematic sections. »*
- **Logique embarquée ESRS** : p. 6. *« ESMA recalls the logic of the ESRS, where this objective is achieved by disclosing policies, actions and targets — or their absence — and metrics linked to the corresponding sustainability matters. »*

###### Description des IRO en SBM-3

- **Exigence** : p. 6. *« ESMA recalls the disclosure requirement in SBM-3 of ESRS 2, including the time horizon associated and whether the IROs arise in the issuer's own operations or in the value chain. »*
- **Recommandation supplémentaire** : p. 6. *« ESMA recommends that this description also include explanations of any interdependence between material IROs, where relevant, and help to ensure that identified positive impacts are not confused with the mitigation of negative impacts. »*

###### Connexion entre IRO et disclosures thématiques

- **Position ESMA** : p. 6-7. *« Comparability between issuers is best achieved when IROs are mapped to ESRS topics and sub-topics and when ESRS terminology is used in the description of IROs, where relevant. »*
- **Outils** : p. 7. *« With the disclosure table required in IRO-2 and the EFRAG explanation on the links between ESRS sustainability matters and disclosure requirements (ID 177 - Links between AR16 and Disclosure requirements). »*
- **Mapping** : p. 7. *« Would also help in identifying the IROs addressed by entity-specific disclosures, as required by SBM-3. »*
- **Bonne pratique encouragée** : p. 7. *« The systematic signposting of entity-specific disclosures for their easier identification within the statement. »*

###### Information non matérielle

- **Position ESMA** : p. 7. *« Non-material information, in cases permitted by the ESRS, should be clearly identified and should not obscure material information. »*

#### J.3 — Données prospectives, plan de transition, scénarios

##### Cibles et ajustement des baselines

- **Position ESMA** : p. 7. *« When the scope of targets has evolved compared to the previous year, issuers should be transparent about any adjustment to the baseline of their targets. »*

#### J.4 — Chaîne de valeur et reporting groupe

##### Périmètre de l'état de durabilité

- **Position ESMA — règle de base** : p. 7. *« Under ESRS 1, the sustainability statement must be for the same reporting entity as the financial statements. »*
- **Confirmation BP-1 ESRS 2** : p. 7. *« For a consolidated sustainability statement, the same scope of consolidation has been used as for the consolidated financial statements. »*
- **Extension de l'information** : p. 7. *« The information provided in the sustainability statement is extended to cover the material IROs connected to the company's value chain, as defined by ESRS 1. »*

##### Limitations de périmètre

- **Transparence requise** : p. 7. *« Where sustainability statements are subject to scope limitations regarding entities in the value chain (paragraph 133 of ESRS 1), ESMA emphasises the need to be fully transparent and to report on all the consequences of these limitations. »*

#### J.5 — Gouvernance de la durabilité

##### Rôle du comité d'audit dans l'état de durabilité

- **Position ESMA — rappel d'ensemble** : p. 1. Le rôle de supervision des comités d'audit est rappelé pour assurer la qualité du rapport financier annuel global (qui inclut l'état de durabilité).

#### J.6 — Articulation avec l'information financière (taxonomie, connectivité)

##### Priorité 2 ESMA — Scope and structure of the sustainability statement (p. 7)

###### Structure en 4 parties prescrite par ESRS 1

- **Cadre ESRS 1** : p. 7. *« Structure in 4 parts (General, Environment, Social, Governance) prescribed by ESRS 1, but with some flexibility in presentation. »*
- **Cross-referencing** : p. 7. *« Can be used to avoid unnecessary duplications and highlight connections between disclosures. »*
- **Équilibre** : p. 7. *« Issuers must strike a balance to ensure that information is not excessively dispersed, as this would contradict the overall objective of readability and clarity. »*

###### Bonne pratique — référence DR

- **Position ESMA** : p. 7. *« ESMA notes that a practical solution for issuers to increase the accessibility and readability of the sustainability statement while using the flexibilities allowed by the ESRS may be to include a reference to the Disclosure Requirement (e.g. "E2-5", "S1-5") when disclosing the associated information. »*
- **Compatibilité digital tagging** : p. 7. *« In this way, disclosures are complete and suitable for the future digital tagging of information. »*
- **Hyperliens** : p. 7. *« ESMA encourages the use of hyperlinks to facilitate internal references. »*

###### Connectivité avec d'autres parties du rapport

- **Exigence ESRS 1** : p. 7. *« ESMA recalls that ESRS 1 requires issuers to illustrate connections with other parts of their corporate reporting. »*
- **Connectivité directe** : p. 7. *« ESMA highlights the ESRS 1 requirement concerning monetary amounts or other quantitative information included in the sustainability statement that are also presented in the financial statements (direct connectivity). »*

##### Section 4 — General considerations (p. 9)

###### Connectivité financière / durabilité

- **Position ESMA** : p. 9. *« ESMA continues to call for consistency and connectivity between climate disclosures in the financial statements and those in the sustainability or management report, as highlighted in previous ECEPs. »*
- **Exemples IASB** : p. 9. **Six near-final illustrative examples published by the IASB on 24 July 2025**, one illustrating how financial and sustainability information can be connected.
- **Application large** : p. 9. *« Although the examples use climate patterns, the principles and requirements illustrated apply equally to other areas of material uncertainties in financial reporting. »*

###### Considérations IFRS et ESEF — futurs développements

- **Amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7** : p. 9. Effectifs **au 1er janvier 2026**, couvrant :
  - *« Reporting on nature-dependent electricity contracts (often structured as power purchase agreements). »*
  - *« Electronic settlement of financial liabilities. »*
  - *« Assessment of the contractual cash flow characteristics of financial assets, including those with ESG-linked features. »*
- **Position ESMA** : p. 9. *« Issuers should promptly analyse the potential impact on the financial statements and disclose any material expected effect in their 2025 financial statements. »*
- **IFRS 18** : p. 9 (note 53 : *« not yet endorsed »*). *« ESMA urges issuers to start assessing the impact of IFRS 18 on financial statements, reporting systems and communications. »* Focus : principes d'agrégation/désagrégation, management-defined performance measures, présentation de la performance financière.

###### Considérations sur les APM (Alternative Performance Measures)

- **Position ESMA** : p. 9. *« APM definitions and calculations must be consistent over time. Caution is advised when adjusting or introducing APMs solely to reflect geopolitical impacts (e.g. customs tariffs). »*
- **Référence** : p. 9, note 56. Paragraph 41 of ESMA Guidelines on APMs.

#### J.7 — Confrontation des positions AMF / ESMA / EFRAG

##### Carry-over des priorités 2024 — 2 priorités sur 4 reprises

  - **Implementation of ESRS requirements on materiality**.
  - **Scope and structure of the sustainability statement**.
- **Motivation** : p. 5. Domaines fondamentaux du reporting de durabilité, encourageant la courbe d'apprentissage des émetteurs tout en reconnaissant que certaines exigences détaillées peuvent être modifiées dans la révision des ESRS.

##### Articulation avec EFRAG

- **EFRAG ID 177** : p. 7, note 45. Links between AR16 and Disclosure requirements.

##### Convergence ESMA / IASB / ISSB

- **Six exemples illustratifs IASB (24 juillet 2025)** : p. 9. *« Demonstrate how financial and sustainability information can be connected. »*

##### Cohérence des hypothèses sur l'ensemble du rapport

## Cartographie des priorités IFRS — Section 1 (hors bloc J)

### Priorité 1 : Geopolitical risks and uncertainties (p. 2-4)

#### Contexte

- **Pertinence** : p. 2. *« Geopolitical risks and uncertainties should be highly relevant for the 2025 financial statements due to their pervasive and multifaceted impact on performance, financial position and disclosures. »*
- **Événements cités** : p. 2. *« War in Ukraine, tensions in the Middle East, increased trade frictions, volatility in energy and commodity prices, supply chain disruptions, changes in global trade patterns. »*

#### Financial implications

- **Énumération** : p. 2.
  - **Potential asset impairments** and write-downs.
  - **Changes in revenue recognition**.
  - **Recoverability** of deferred tax assets.
  - **Reassessment** of provisioning needs, liquidity risks, key assumptions of valuation models (ECL, fair-value).
  - **Compliance** with debt covenants.
  - **Going concern assumptions** and sensitivity analyses.

#### Position ESMA sur les disclosures

- **Spécificité requise** : p. 2. *« Issuers should avoid generic references to "geopolitical uncertainties" and instead be specific about developments or events affecting their judgements and assumptions (including going concern). »*
- **Analyses de sensibilité** : p. 2. *« ESMA reminds issuers to provide sensitivity analyses illustrating how carrying amounts would be affected by reasonably possible changes in these assumptions during the next financial year. »*
- **Additional disclosures** : p. 3. *« Issuers should apply judgement and consider all facts and circumstances to determine whether additional disclosures (beyond those explicitly required by IFRS) are necessary. »*

#### Write-down of inventories and impairment of non-financial assets

- **IAS 2** : p. 2. Recognition and disclosure of write-downs of inventories (especially work in progress).
- **IAS 36** : p. 2-3. Impairment tests and critical review of assumptions.

#### Deferred tax assets (DTA)

- **IAS 12** : p. 3. Reassessment of DTA recognition and measurement.

#### Revenue recognition

- **IFRS 15** : p. 3. Over-time recognition, cost-based input method, onerous contracts.
- **Renegotiation** : p. 3. Contract modifications under IFRS 15.

#### Restructuring provisions

- **IAS 37 and IFRS 5** : p. 3. Provisions for restructuring costs and non-current assets held for sale.

#### Financial instruments

- **IFRS 9** : p. 3-4. Assessment of effects on credit risk, ECL on loans and trade and lease receivables.

### Priorité 2 : Segment reporting (p. 4)

#### Principes généraux d'identification et d'agrégation

- **IFRS 8** : p. 4. Disclosure enabling users to evaluate the nature and financial effects of business activities.
- **Cohérence requise** : p. 4. *« Between segment information in the management report, in reporting to the CODM (Chief Operating Decision Maker) and in the financial statements. »*
- **Aggregation criteria** : p. 4. *« Criteria (a)-(e) of paragraph 12 of IFRS 8 can only be applied to operating segments with similar economic characteristics. »*
- **Position ESMA** : p. 4. *« Geopolitical uncertainties (e.g. trade frictions) and climate matters may lead to changes in issuers' operating segments, the application of aggregation criteria and/or the disaggregation of revenues. »*

#### Disclosure of revenues and expenses for reportable segments

- **IFRS IC agenda decision (July 2024)** : p. 4. Le paragraphe 23 d'IFRS 8 requiert *« the disclosure of certain items of revenue and expenses if they are: (i) included in the measure of segment profit/loss reviewed by the CODM (even if not separately provided or reviewed), or (ii) regularly provided to the CODM (even if not included in the segment measure). »*
- **Material items** : p. 4. *« Material items of revenue and expenses to be disclosed under paragraph 23(f) of IFRS 8 are not limited to unusual or non-recurring items. »*

#### Information on geographical areas and major customers

- **IFRS 8 §33-34** : p. 4. *« Entity-wide disclosures on geographical areas and major customers particularly relevant in the current environment of trade barriers and geopolitical uncertainty. »*
- **Expected disclosures** : p. 4. Revenus de clients externes attribués à et certains actifs non courants situés dans :
  - *« (i) the country of domicile of the entity. »*
  - *« (ii) all foreign countries in total. »*
  - *« (iii) individual countries, if material. »*
- **Materiality** : p. 4. *« Consider quantitative and qualitative factors. The fact that all material revenues are attributed to foreign countries may be material information (especially if these countries are subject to trade restrictions or tariffs). »*
- **No exemption for major customers** : p. 4. *« Paragraph 34 of IFRS 8 does not provide for exemptions from disclosing information on revenues concerning major customers. »*

## Priorité ESEF — Section 3 (p. 8)

### Common errors in the statement of cash flows

- **Focus** : p. 8. *« For the examination of the 2025 annual financial reports subject to ESEF requirements with consolidated financial statements, ESMA and enforcers will pay particular attention to common ESEF errors found in the cash flow statement. »*

### Five areas of errors identified

- **1. Correctness of mark-ups** : p. 8. *« IFRS taxonomy concepts in the cash flow statement misapplied (e.g. marking "Net cash flow from operations" with a financing activity concept). »*
- **2. Completeness and consistency of markups** : p. 8. *« Incomplete or inconsistent tagging of financial items, especially for comparative periods or for declared currency numbers included in the notes. »*
- **3. Structural, presentation and calculation correctness** : p. 8. ESMA exhorte à suivre les règles de construction technique du RTS on ESEF et de l'ESEF Reporting Manual. *« Common errors include storing all linkbases in one file. »*
- **4. Extension taxonomy elements & anchoring** : p. 8. *« Unnecessary extensions often deriving from a lack of knowledge of the complete IFRS taxonomy. Extension elements must be anchored to an appropriate core taxonomy element. »*
- **5. Signs, Scales & Numerical Representation** : p. 8. *« Incorrect signs (e.g. cash outflows as negative values), the decimals attribute often misused, scaling errors. »*

## Pistes et positions de l'ESMA en 2025

L'ESMA, dans cette ECEP 2025, exprime :

1. **Aux émetteurs « Wave 1 »** : *« do not rely on the EFRAG draft but follow the "Quick Fix" Delegated Act to determine 2026 obligations on 2025 financial year »* (p. 5).
2. **Aux émetteurs « Wave 2 »** : *« follow the transposition of the "Stop the clock" Directive in their Member States »* (p. 5).
3. **À tous les émetteurs** : *« carry-over of the two priorities from the 2024 ECEP (materiality + scope/structure) to allow progression on the learning curve »* (p. 5).
4. **Sur la Taxonomy** : pas de recommandation spécifique en 2025, en raison du changement d'environnement réglementaire (p. 5).
5. **Sur la connectivité financière/durabilité** : *« continuous call for consistency and use of the six IASB illustrative examples of 24 July 2025 »* (p. 9).
