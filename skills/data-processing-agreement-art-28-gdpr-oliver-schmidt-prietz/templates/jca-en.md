# Joint Controller Arrangement Template — English

This template implements the requirements of Art. 26 GDPR for joint controllers. It is **not** an Art. 28 / DPA template — the obligations under Art. 28(3)(a)–(h) do not apply between joint controllers.

If the relationship turns out to be controller–processor (not joint control), use one of the controller-processor templates instead — `templates/dpa-commercial-en.md` (Tier 1), `templates/dpa-strict-en.md` (Tier 2, 2021/915 unmodified), or `templates/dpa-hybrid-en.md` (Tier 3, Sections I+II of 2021/915 + custom Section III). See `references/tier-selection.md` for tier choice. Run the screen test in `references/art26-joint-controller.md` before using this template.

# JOINT CONTROLLER ARRANGEMENT

This **Joint Controller Arrangement** (the "**JCA**") is entered into on `{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}` between:

**`{{CONTROLLER_A_NAME}}`**, a `{{LEGAL_FORM}}` registered under the laws of `{{JURISDICTION}}` with registered seat at `{{ADDRESS}}` and registration number `{{REG_NO}}`

("**Controller A**")

— and —

**`{{CONTROLLER_B_NAME}}`**, a `{{LEGAL_FORM}}` registered under the laws of `{{JURISDICTION}}` with registered seat at `{{ADDRESS}}` and registration number `{{REG_NO}}`

("**Controller B**", together with Controller A the "**Joint Controllers**" and each a "**Party**").

## Recitals

A. The Joint Controllers have entered into `{{UNDERLYING_ARRANGEMENT}}` (the "**Underlying Arrangement**") under which they jointly carry out the processing of Personal Data described in **Annex B** (Description of Joint Processing).

B. The Joint Controllers determine jointly the purposes and means of the processing in scope of this JCA and are therefore joint controllers within the meaning of Article 26(1) GDPR.

C. This JCA sets out the Joint Controllers' respective responsibilities for compliance with the GDPR pursuant to Article 26(1), and provides the essence of the arrangement to be made available to data subjects pursuant to Article 26(2) by way of the public summary set out in **Annex C**.

D. Nothing in this JCA limits a data subject's right under Article 26(3) GDPR to exercise rights under the GDPR against either Joint Controller.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this JCA:

- "**Allocation Matrix**" means the matrix in **Annex A** allocating compliance responsibilities between the Joint Controllers.
- "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means the GDPR and any other data protection law applicable to the processing.
- "**Joint Processing**" means the processing operations described in **Annex B** that the Joint Controllers carry out jointly.
- "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in Article 4(1) GDPR.
- "**Personal Data Breach**" has the meaning given in Article 4(12) GDPR.
- "**Public Summary**" means the summary of this JCA in **Annex C**, made available to data subjects pursuant to Article 26(2) GDPR.
- Other capitalised terms used but not defined in this JCA have the meaning given in the GDPR.

## 2. Subject matter and scope

2.1 The Joint Processing in scope of this JCA is described in **Annex B**.

2.2 Each Joint Controller may carry out **separate processing** of Personal Data outside the scope of the Joint Processing for its own purposes. Such separate processing is not governed by this JCA. Each Joint Controller is solely responsible for compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law in respect of its separate processing.

2.3 Where a Joint Controller processes Personal Data in the role of processor for the other Joint Controller (e.g. operational support services), such processing shall be governed by a separate Data Processing Agreement under Art. 28 GDPR. This JCA does not regulate such processor relationships.

## 3. Lawful bases and purposes

3.1 The respective purposes pursued by each Joint Controller in the Joint Processing are set out in **Annex B**.

3.2 Each Joint Controller is responsible for ensuring that it has a valid lawful basis under Article 6 GDPR (and, where applicable, Article 9 GDPR) for its participation in the Joint Processing.

## 4. Allocation of responsibilities

4.1 The Joint Controllers' respective responsibilities for compliance with the GDPR are allocated as set out in the **Allocation Matrix** in **Annex A**.

4.2 The Allocation Matrix establishes, for each compliance topic, whether the responsibility lies with Controller A, Controller B, or both jointly. Where the Allocation Matrix designates a Joint Controller as primarily responsible, that Joint Controller shall lead in respect of that topic; the other Joint Controller shall cooperate as needed and provide relevant information.

4.3 The allocation of responsibilities under the Allocation Matrix is internal between the Joint Controllers. It does not limit a data subject's right under Article 26(3) GDPR to exercise rights against either Joint Controller, and it does not limit either Joint Controller's external accountability under the GDPR.

## 5. Information duties (Articles 13 and 14 GDPR)

5.1 The Joint Controller designated in the Allocation Matrix as primarily responsible for information duties shall draft and maintain the privacy notice covering the Joint Processing.

5.2 The privacy notice shall include the Public Summary in **Annex C** or a clear reference to it.

5.3 The other Joint Controller shall provide all information necessary for the privacy notice and shall publish or signpost the same Public Summary in its own privacy notice.

5.4 Material changes to the Joint Processing that affect the privacy notice shall be discussed between the Joint Controllers in advance.

## 6. Data subjects' rights

6.1 The primary contact for data subjects in respect of rights relating to the Joint Processing shall be `{{Controller A / Controller B / either, as designated in the Allocation Matrix}}` (the "**Primary Contact**"), with contact details set out in **Annex C**.

6.2 If a data subject contacts a Joint Controller other than the Primary Contact, that Joint Controller shall promptly forward the request to the Primary Contact, while preserving the data subject's right under Article 26(3) to receive a response from the Joint Controller they contacted.

6.3 The Joint Controllers shall cooperate to respond to data subject requests within the deadlines under Article 12(3) GDPR. Each Joint Controller shall provide the other with the information and assistance necessary to respond.

6.4 The Allocation Matrix specifies which Joint Controller bears primary operational responsibility for each category of right (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, automated decision-making).

## 7. Personal Data Breach

7.1 Each Joint Controller shall notify the other without undue delay and in any event within `{{24 / 48}}` hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data Breach affecting the Joint Processing. The notification shall include the elements set out in Article 33(3) GDPR to the extent reasonably available.

7.2 The Joint Controller designated in the Allocation Matrix as primarily responsible for breach notification to the supervisory authority shall lead the Article 33 notification, including drafting and submission, with input from the other Joint Controller.

7.3 The Joint Controller designated in the Allocation Matrix as primarily responsible for communication to data subjects shall lead the Article 34 communication where required.

7.4 The Joint Controllers shall cooperate in the assessment of the Personal Data Breach, the determination of necessary remedial action, and the documentation of the breach for Article 33(5) records.

## 8. Security of processing (Article 32 GDPR)

8.1 Each Joint Controller is responsible for implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures for the systems under its control that participate in the Joint Processing.

8.2 Where the Joint Processing relies on shared infrastructure or interfaces, the Joint Controllers shall coordinate to ensure consistent security measures and shall jointly determine the appropriate level of security taking into account Article 32(1) and (2) GDPR.

8.3 Each Joint Controller's relevant security measures are summarised in `{{a separate document referenced here / in the Underlying Arrangement / and may be made available to the other on request}}`.

## 9. Data protection impact assessment (Article 35) and prior consultation (Article 36)

9.1 Where the Joint Processing requires a data protection impact assessment under Article 35 GDPR, the Joint Controllers shall jointly conduct it. The Joint Controller designated in the Allocation Matrix shall lead the drafting; the other Joint Controller shall provide input and review.

9.2 Where prior consultation with a supervisory authority under Article 36 GDPR is required, the Joint Controllers shall coordinate the consultation.

9.3 Material changes to the Joint Processing shall be assessed jointly for any new DPIA requirement before implementation.

## 10. Records of processing (Article 30)

10.1 Each Joint Controller shall maintain its own records of processing under Article 30 GDPR.

10.2 The Joint Controllers shall exchange the information necessary to keep their records consistent in respect of the Joint Processing.

## 11. International transfers

11.1 If the Joint Processing involves transfers of Personal Data outside the EEA, the Joint Controllers shall jointly determine the transfer mechanism (adequacy, SCCs, BCRs, or derogation) and shall maintain a joint Transfer Impact Assessment where required.

11.2 Each Joint Controller is responsible for executing the relevant transfer instruments for transfers it makes from its own systems.

## 12. Sub-processors and vendors

12.1 Each Joint Controller may engage its own processors (sub-processors / vendors) under separate Art. 28 contracts for the operational support of its participation in the Joint Processing.

12.2 The Joint Controllers shall disclose to each other the processors involved in the Joint Processing where reasonably relevant for compliance and operational coordination. A list of such processors is maintained in **Annex D**.

12.3 This Section 12 does not flow Article 28 obligations between the Joint Controllers — the Joint Controllers do not act as processors for each other.

## 13. Liability

13.1 As between the Joint Controllers internally:

(a) Each Joint Controller bears responsibility for damages, regulatory fines, and data subject claims to the extent caused by its own breach of Applicable Data Protection Law or this JCA.

(b) Where joint conduct contributed to a damage, the Joint Controllers shall allocate the costs in proportion to their respective responsibility.

13.2 Article 26(3) GDPR provides that data subjects may exercise their rights against either Joint Controller irrespective of the internal allocation in this JCA. Each Joint Controller acknowledges that it may be required to respond to data subjects and supervisory authorities even for matters where the other Joint Controller bears primary internal responsibility.

13.3 Where one Joint Controller pays a data subject claim or regulatory fine that is, under Section 13.1, internally the other Joint Controller's responsibility, the paying Joint Controller may seek contribution from the other.

## 14. Cooperation with supervisory authorities

14.1 Each Joint Controller has its own competent supervisory authority. Where the Joint Processing is the subject of a regulatory enquiry or proceeding, the Joint Controllers shall cooperate, including by sharing relevant information and coordinating responses.

## 15. Public summary (Article 26(2))

15.1 The Public Summary in **Annex C** sets out the essence of this JCA in plain language.

15.2 Each Joint Controller shall make the Public Summary available to data subjects, in particular by including or referencing it in its privacy notice.

15.3 Material changes to this JCA that affect the Public Summary shall trigger a corresponding update of the Public Summary, which each Joint Controller shall publish promptly.

## 16. Term, termination, post-termination

16.1 This JCA shall enter into force on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for the duration of the Joint Processing.

16.2 Either Joint Controller may terminate this JCA on `{{60}}` days' written notice. Termination of this JCA does not by itself terminate the Underlying Arrangement; the Joint Controllers shall agree on the consequences for the Underlying Arrangement.

16.3 Upon termination of the Joint Processing, the Joint Controllers shall agree on the disposition of the Personal Data, in particular:

(a) which Joint Controller retains custody of which data;

(b) deletion of duplicates not required for ongoing separate processing;

(c) ongoing handling of data subject rights requests relating to the past Joint Processing.

16.4 Article 5(1)(e), Article 17, and Article 32 GDPR continue to apply to each Joint Controller's residual data after termination.

## 17. Amendments and notices

17.1 Amendments to this JCA shall be in writing and signed by both Joint Controllers.

17.2 Notices shall be given in writing to the addresses at the head of this JCA, or to such other address as a Joint Controller may notify to the other in writing.

## 18. Severability

18.1 If any provision of this JCA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

## 19. Governing law and jurisdiction

19.1 This JCA shall be governed by the law of `{{GOVERNING_LAW}}`. The courts of `{{VENUE}}` shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 20. Order of precedence

20.1 In case of conflict between this JCA and the Underlying Arrangement on data protection matters, this JCA shall prevail.

# Practitioner's note (for OneZero Legal client deliverables)

This template implements Art. 26 GDPR for a typical bilateral joint-controller arrangement. Adjustments may be needed for:
- Tripartite or multi-party joint controllership (extend the Allocation Matrix and signatories).
- Asymmetric arrangements (one party clearly leading; the matrix may concentrate responsibility).
- Group-internal joint controllership (consider a master JCA with annexes per processing operation).
- Specific sectoral overlays.

After execution: the **Public Summary in Annex C must be published**. Privacy notices on both Joint Controllers' websites should include or signpost the summary. This is a standalone deliverable — do not consider the JCA project complete until the Public Summary is live.
