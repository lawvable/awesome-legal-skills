# Verification workflow — KSC

The step-by-step procedure for verifying a KSC citation before it appears in an output.

## The principle

No case-specific citation appears in an output until it has been verified against an authoritative source in the current conversation. Foundational instruments (Law 05/L-053, Article 162 Constitution, RPE, Host State Agreement) may be cited from project knowledge when present.

## The fallback ladder

1. **scp-ks.org** — the official KSC website. Actively maintained. First and primary source.
2. **legal-tools.org** — ICC Legal Tools Database. Mirrors KSC principal decisions.
3. **Council of Europe** (assembly.coe.int) — for the Marty Report and PACE Resolutions.
4. **Tier 2 scholarship** — for context and doctrinal commentary; never for tribunal findings.
5. **Ask the user** if nothing verifies.

## Capture these fields

- **Case number** — KSC-BC-2020-04, KSC-BC-2020-05, KSC-BC-2020-06, etc.
- **Document type** — Indictment (confirmed/amended), Decision, Trial Judgment, Sentencing Judgment, Reparation Order, Appeal Judgment
- **Panel/chamber** — Pre-Trial Judge, Trial Panel I, Trial Panel II, Court of Appeals Panel, Supreme Court Panel, Constitutional Court Panel
- **Date** — exact date of issuance
- **Paragraph number(s)** — for pinpoint citations
- **Public vs confidential / redacted version** — use public versions; the operative indictment in Thaçi et al. is the **lesser-redacted version of 27 February 2023**

## Match the verification level to the claim

- "Mustafa was convicted by the KSC Trial Panel on 16 December 2022" → existence verified
- "The Trial Panel held that the killings at Zllash were part of a widespread or systematic attack…" → content verified
- A direct quotation from the Mustafa Trial Judgment → paragraph verified

Never state a higher level than was actually reached.

## KSC-specific traps

### Trap 1 — KSC is a Kosovo court, not an international tribunal

The KSC is constitutionally and legally a **Kosovo court** (Article 162 of the Kosovo Constitution; Law 05/L-053). Calling it "an international tribunal" is imprecise. The KSC applies Kosovo law (informed by international law); its judges are international; but the institution itself is Kosovar.

The accurate framing: **hybrid court / internationalised national court**. Not: "international tribunal".

### Trap 2 — "Statute" vs "Law"

The KSC has **no separate Statute**. Law 05/L-053 is the equivalent of a Statute. Citing "the KSC Statute" is inaccurate; cite "the Law" or "Law 05/L-053".

### Trap 3 — Thaçi et al. is *awaiting judgment*

As of early 2026, the Thaçi et al. case (KSC-BC-2020-06) is in the post-closing-statements phase, awaiting judgment. Hashim Thaçi has not been convicted of any crime. Care with framing — the accused are presumed innocent until conviction. The arrests occurred November 2020, indictment confirmed October 2020, trial began April 2023, closing statements February 2026.

### Trap 4 — Multiple Thaçi et al. cases

There are now **two distinct Thaçi-headed cases**:
- **KSC-BC-2020-06** — the principal war crimes / crimes against humanity case (Thaçi, Veseli, Selimi, Krasniqi)
- **KSC-BC-2024-11** — an administration of justice case (Thaçi, Smakaj, Kilaj, Fazliu, Kuçi) — arrests December 2024, trial commenced February 2026

When the user refers to "the Thaçi case", clarify which.

### Trap 5 — Reparation orders are a substantive feature

The KSC orders reparations directly against convicted persons (Law 05/L-053 Arts. 21-22; RPE Rule 168). The Shala Reparation Order of €208,000 (29 November 2024) is the leading example. This is distinct from the STL model (no reparations) and the ICC model (TFV-managed reparations).

### Trap 6 — Marty Report is parliamentary, not judicial

The 7 January 2011 Marty Report is a **Council of Europe Parliamentary Assembly document**, not a judicial finding. When citing the Marty Report in the context of KSC jurisdiction (Law 05/L-053 narrows KSC competence to the allegations in the Report), note its status as a parliamentary inquiry document.

## Translation discipline

KSC proceedings operate in **English, Albanian, and Serbian**. All public judgments and decisions are published in all three. The **English version is conventionally treated as authoritative for international scholarship**. Where the original speaker spoke Albanian or Serbian, the original-language version may matter for interpretation. The Law 05/L-053 exists in Albanian and Serbian as the original languages; English is an official translation.

## Partial verification is acceptable if disclosed

If you can confirm the existence and substance but not the paragraph, the honest output says so. Disclosed partial verification is acceptable; silent overstatement is not.
