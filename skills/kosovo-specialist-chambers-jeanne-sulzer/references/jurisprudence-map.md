# Jurisprudence map — KSC

A topic-by-topic map of the KSC's principal holdings as of 2026. The KSC's jurisprudence is **actively developing** — verify the most recent state of any case before citing.

## 1. Institutional architecture (settled)

- KSC is a **Kosovo court** with international staffing, located in The Hague (Article 162 of the Constitution; Law 05/L-053; Host State Agreement 2016)
- Four-tier chamber architecture: Basic Court / Court of Appeals / Supreme Court / Constitutional Court
- SPO is an independent prosecutorial organ
- Detention facilities at Scheveningen, The Hague
- Reparation orders against convicted persons directly (a feature distinct from the STL and the ad hoc tribunals)

## 2. The Marty Report and KSC jurisdiction

The Council of Europe Parliamentary Assembly Report by Senator Dick Marty (Doc. 12462, 7 January 2011), titled "Inhuman treatment of people and illicit trafficking in human organs in Kosovo", forms the **factual frame** for KSC jurisdiction. Law 05/L-053 Articles 6-12 confine KSC competence to crimes alleged in the Marty Report's context, committed in Kosovo (and at related sites in Albania) between 1 January 1998 and 31 December 2000.

The Marty Report is a parliamentary inquiry document, not a judicial finding. When cited, identify its parliamentary status.

## 3. The first KSC trial judgments — settled jurisprudence

### Mustafa (KSC-BC-2020-05)

- **Trial Judgment**, Trial Panel I, **16 December 2022**
- **Appeal Judgment**, Court of Appeals Panel, **14 December 2023**
- The first KSC trial judgment. Salih Mustafa convicted of war crimes (arbitrary detention, torture, murder) in the context of a non-international armed conflict in Kosovo.
- Establishes the KSC's framework for proving the existence of a non-international armed conflict and the elements of war crimes under Kosovo and customary international law.

### Shala (KSC-BC-2020-04)

- **Trial Judgment**, Trial Panel I, **16 July 2024**
- **Reparation Order**, Trial Panel I, **29 November 2024** — Shala ordered to pay €208,000 in compensation to victims for physical, mental and material harm
- **Appeal Judgment**, Court of Appeals Panel, **14 July 2025** — affirmed conviction on Counts 1 (arbitrary detention), 3 (torture), 4 (murder); reduced sentence from 18 to 13 years on grounds raised in three of fourteen appeal points
- Establishes the leading practice on **reparation orders** at the KSC. Eight participating victims in the case.

### Gucati and Haradinaj (KSC-BC-2020-07)

- **Trial Judgment**, Trial Panel I, **18 May 2022**
- **Appeal Judgment**, Court of Appeals Panel, **2 February 2023** — sentences reduced
- The first KSC contempt-of-court / administration-of-justice case. Convictions for obstruction of justice, intimidation during criminal proceedings, and violation of the secrecy of proceedings.
- Significant for KSC contempt jurisprudence; foreshadows the Thaçi et al. (administration of justice) case at KSC-BC-2024-11.

## 4. Pending — Thaçi et al. (KSC-BC-2020-06)

- Opening statements **3 April 2023**
- SPO closed its case **15 April 2025**
- Defence Preparation Conference **19 August 2025**
- Defence case commenced **15 September 2025** (only Thaçi and Krasniqi Defence presented evidence; Veseli and Selimi did not)
- Both Defence cases concluded **2 December 2025**
- Evidentiary proceedings declared closed **18 December 2025**
- Closing statements **9-18 February 2026**
- **Judgment pending as of 2026**
- 155 participating victims in the case
- Six counts of crimes against humanity: persecution, imprisonment, other inhumane acts, torture, murder, enforced disappearance of persons
- The KLA's General Staff (Thaçi, Veseli, Selimi, Krasniqi) face charges based on multiple modes of individual criminal responsibility

**Treat all framing of guilt as alleged**. The accused are presumed innocent until conviction.

## 5. Substantive doctrine — emerging KSC jurisprudence

### Non-international armed conflict in Kosovo, 1998-2000

The KSC has (in *Mustafa* and *Shala*) made factual and legal findings on the existence of a non-international armed conflict in Kosovo between the KLA and FRY/Serbian forces. The relevant period and the geographic scope of the armed conflict are established through detailed factual findings.

### Modes of individual criminal responsibility

Law 05/L-053 Art. 16 frames the modes available. The KSC has applied joint criminal enterprise (drawing on Tadić-line ICTY jurisprudence), command responsibility, and direct perpetration in the cases decided.

### Reparations to victims

The KSC's reparation jurisdiction (Law 05/L-053 Arts. 21-22; RPE Rule 168) is exercised directly against convicted persons. The **Shala Reparation Order of 29 November 2024** is the leading judgment to date. The order specifies the heads of harm (physical, mental, material), the amount (€208,000), and the procedures for execution. This is a distinctively KSC feature among hybrid tribunals.

### Witness intimidation and contempt

The KSC's contempt jurisprudence is developing rapidly. *Gucati and Haradinaj* (KSC-BC-2020-07) is the leading authority. The administration-of-justice case at KSC-BC-2024-11 will further develop the doctrine.

### Victim participation under Rule 113

Rule 113 governs the granting of victim status and the modalities of participation. The Victims' Counsel represents groups of victims at trial. 155 participating victims in Thaçi et al. is a record for the KSC and a substantial victim-participation case by international standards.

## 6. Constitutional Court Chamber

A unique KSC feature: the **Constitutional Court Chamber** handles constitutional referrals concerning the KSC. Decisions of the Constitutional Court Chamber on procedural and structural questions (privileges, immunities, jurisdiction challenges) form a parallel body of KSC jurisprudence that is institutionally significant though less voluminous than the criminal jurisprudence.
