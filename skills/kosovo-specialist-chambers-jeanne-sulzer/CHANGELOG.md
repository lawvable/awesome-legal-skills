### Skill scope at v1.0.0
- Covers the KSC and SPO as actively operating institutions (the KSC is the most active hybrid tribunal as of 2026)
- Encodes the same verification-first methodology as the other skills
- Specifically equips the user to handle the KSC's institutional novelty (Kosovo court relocated to The Hague; no Statute; reparation orders against convicted persons; Constitutional Court Chamber)
- Maps the four concluded cases (Mustafa, Shala, Gucati/Haradinaj, Januzi/Bahtijari/Haxhi Shala) and the two ongoing/pending cases (Thaçi et al. principal, Thaçi et al. administration of justice)

### Known limitations
- The KSC is actively operating; the case status changes regularly. Verify against scp-ks.org for current state of any specific case before citing.
- The Thaçi et al. judgment will, when issued, substantially develop KSC jurisprudence on crimes against humanity and modes of liability. A v1.1 release will follow the judgment.
- KSC procedural decisions are voluminous; the skill maps principal decisions only. Specific interlocutory matters require case-by-case verification.
