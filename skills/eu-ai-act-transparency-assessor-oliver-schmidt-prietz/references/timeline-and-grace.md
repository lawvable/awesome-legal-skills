## Statutory baseline — 2 August 2026

Art. 50 (Chapter IV transparency) is **not** a high-risk obligation and is **not** postponed by the
Digital Omnibus. It runs on the Art. 113 **general application date of 2 August 2026**.

Two common misfilings to avoid:
- filing Art. 50 under the postponed Annex III high-risk date (2 Dec 2027) — **wrong**, Art. 50 is not high-risk;
- filing it under the 2 Aug 2025 GPAI/governance tranche (Art. 113(b)) — **wrong**, Art. 50 is not in that tranche.

From 2 Aug 2026, with **no transition**: 50(1) interaction disclosure, 50(3) emotion/biometric notice,
50(4) deepfake/public-interest-text labelling, and 50(2) marking for any system **newly placed on the
market** on/after that date.

## The one Omnibus relief — legacy 50(2) marking → 2 December 2026 (ADOPTED, awaiting OJ)

The **only** Digital Omnibus relief touching Art. 50 is a grace for **legacy** generative-AI marking under
50(2): systems on the market **before** 2 Aug 2026 get until **2 December 2026** to implement machine-readable
marking. The Omnibus **cut the grace from six months to three** (the Commission had originally proposed
2 Feb 2027; the co-legislators settled on 2 Dec 2026).

> **Do not conflate the two "2 Feb 2027"s.** 2 Feb 2027 is *not* the legacy-marking date (that is
> 2 Dec 2026). 2 Feb 2027 is separately the *Code's watermark-detection interoperability* milestone, and
> was also the Commission's *superseded original* legacy-marking proposal — two different things that share a date.

**Status as of 2026-07-04 — "open issue" until OJ, but far past "politically agreed":**
The Digital Omnibus on AI ("Omnibus VII") has cleared both co-legislators. European Parliament adopted it
in June 2026; the **Council gave its final green light on 29 June 2026**. Per the Council, "the legislative
act will be published in the EU's Official Journal **shortly** and will enter into force on the **third day
after** this publication." So the 2 Dec 2026 grace is **adopted and imminent**, not merely "proposed" — but
it is **not yet black-letter law** until the OJ text appears.

**Planning rule for the skill:** present 2 Dec 2026 as a **near-settled** legacy 50(2) date (adopted by
Parliament + Council; awaiting OJ). Add a light caveat — *until the OJ text is published, the statutory
2 Aug 2026 date formally still stands for legacy systems* — and recommend a quick live OJ / law-tracker
check (procedure 2025/359). This is a **weaker** caveat than the earlier "politically agreed / may not
happen" framing; do not overstate the residual risk.

## Other Digital Omnibus changes (boundary awareness)

The Omnibus does more than the 50(2) grace. These do **not** change Art. 50 itself but a user may raise them:

- **High-risk application deferred:** stand-alone Annex III high-risk → **2 Dec 2027**; high-risk embedded in
  products → **2 Aug 2028**. (Art. 50 is **not** high-risk and is **not** deferred — it still runs on 2 Aug 2026.)
- **New Art. 5 prohibitions** on generating **non-consensual intimate / sexual deepfakes** and **CSAM**
  (nudification apps), applying from **December 2026**. This is an Art. 5 *prohibition*, not an Art. 50
  transparency duty — if a use falls here, the prohibition governs and a 50(4) label cannot cure it (mirrors
  the 50(3) ↔ Art. 5 logic).
- **Sandbox deadline** for national authorities postponed to **2 Aug 2027**.
- **Adequacy-assessment procedure** simplified: a single adequacy assessment with a Commission opinion after
  consulting the AI Board now produces the same legal effect (relevant to the Code's pending assessment).

## No retrospective marking

Outputs already in the information ecosystem **before 2 August 2026** do **not** have to be marked or
labelled retrospectively (draft Commission Guidelines). Voluntary marking is encouraged. The duties bite
for content generated/published from the application date onward.

## Roadmap shape (use in Phase 5 output)

1. **Now → 22 Jul 2026** — decide on Code signatory intent; if signing, submit the form by the deadline.
2. **Now → 2 Aug 2026** — implement 50(1) disclosure, 50(3) notices, 50(4) labelling, and 50(2) marking
   for new systems. No transition — these are hard.
3. **By 2 Dec 2026 (adopted, awaiting OJ)** — complete 50(2) marking for legacy generative systems. The
   grace is adopted by Parliament + Council (29 Jun 2026); confirm the OJ publication date. Until published,
   the statutory 2 Aug 2026 date formally still stands for legacy systems.
4. **By 2 Feb 2027** — meet the Code's watermark-detection interoperability obligation (if a signatory /
   relying on the Code as the compliance route for 50(2)).
