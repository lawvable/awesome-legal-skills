## How to use this table

1. **For navigation only.** This is the "Frequently cited authorities" reference at the front of the Guide Vol 2. It tells you the document exists at the given number on the given date.
2. **For substantive citation, verify.** Before citing a paragraph or holding from any of these documents, fetch the document itself via `eccc.gov.kh` or `legal-tools.org` and verify both the proposition and the paragraph reference.
3. **For doctrinal questions, consult the Guide Vol 2.** Each chapter of the Guide cross-references these documents with footnoted paragraph anchors. For example, on personal jurisdiction the Guide cites Case 001 Appeal Judgment paras 45-79; on Case 001 Closing Order para. 129; and so on.

## Documents not in this quick reference

The "Frequently cited authorities" table covers the principal Closing Orders, Trial Judgments, and Appeal Judgments. Many other documents are cited throughout the Guide Vol 2 — interlocutory decisions, transcripts, expert reports, witness statements (the E3/[number] exhibit series). For these, work the verification ladder in `verification-workflow.md`.
