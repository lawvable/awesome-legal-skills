**Provisos**

\* **Data freshness.** Excel rows reflect the UNCTAD ISDS Navigator full-data Excel, snapshot 31/12/2023 (1,332 treaty-based cases; the live Navigator last reported 1,463 as of 31/12/2025). The post-2023 layer comes from her ICSID profile (retrieved 2026-08-02; self-reported; last updated by the arbitrator 16 Sep 2024) and therefore captures **ICSID cases only** — any post-2023 UNCITRAL, PCA, or SCC appointments are not visible from compliant free sources and nothing is asserted about them. The ICSID profile also lists ~12 contract-based or annulment-phase cases outside UNCTAD's treaty-based scope; they are disclosed here but excluded from the counts. Combined totals marked * mix the two sources and carry both limitations.

\*\* **Dissent counting.** An arbitrator is counted as dissenting only where a published individual opinion names them. Silence in a case with recorded opinions data is treated as no published opinion — it does not prove the decision was unanimous. The denominator (91) counts cases with at least one substantive decision (award, or decision on jurisdiction, merits/liability, or quantum) — consent awards, procedural orders, and discontinuances are excluded as not dissentable.

† **Outcomes are tribunal-level.** "Breach found" describes what the tribunal decided, not how this arbitrator voted; individual positions are confirmed only from the decisions and any separate opinions ("check the record on her votes").

‡ **Scope.** Treaty-based ISDS only; commercial-arbitration appointments and confidential cases are invisible; counts cannot be deemed exhaustive. Fuller analytics exist in subscription databases (Jus Mundi, GAR ART) not used by this tool. Not legal advice; research aid for counsel.
