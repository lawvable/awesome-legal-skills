# Authoritative sources — Reg. 64 Kosovo Panels

## Tier 1 — primary, authoritative

### unmik.unmissions.org — UNMIK official UN website

The principal source for **UNMIK Regulations** including 2000/6, 2000/64, 2001/2, 2003/25, 2003/26. Navigate to "Regulations" or "Documents". Available in English; some materials also in Albanian and Serbian.

### unmikonline.org — UNMIK legacy site

The historical UNMIK administration site, intermittently available. Also hosts regulations and many procedural documents from the active UNMIK administration period (1999-2008). Check the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) when the site is unavailable.

### EULEX Kosovo archives — eulex-kosovo.eu

For the **2008-2018 Executive Mandate period** and continuing institutional record. EULEX took over international judicial functions from UNMIK on 9 December 2008. The EULEX archive contains:
- Press releases on major cases tried by EULEX international judges
- Mission reports
- Some judgment summaries (full judgments typically on Kosovo Supreme Court / district court archives)

### legal-tools.org — ICC Legal Tools Database

Mirrors:
- All UNMIK Regulations
- Selected Reg. 64 Panel judgments
- The Provisional Criminal Code and Provisional Criminal Procedure Code (UNMIK Regs. 2003/25, 2003/26)

Navigate via "Other International(ised) Criminal Jurisdictions" > "Kosovo" (covering both UNMIK Reg. 64 Panels and EULEX successor proceedings).

### UN Security Council archives

For:
- **UNSC Resolution 1244 (1999)** of 10 June 1999 — the founding instrument of UNMIK
- Subsequent SC resolutions on Kosovo
- Reports of the Secretary-General on UNMIK (periodic)
- EULEX-related SC documents

Available at un.org/securitycouncil and via the UN Digital Library.

### Supreme Court of Kosovo and Kosovo judicial archives

For Kosovo Albanian-language judgments from Reg. 64 Panels and successor proceedings. The current Kosovo judicial council (Kosovo Judicial Council, gjyqesori-rks.org) maintains archives of district court and Supreme Court records, including many UNMIK-era judgments. Search may require Albanian-language navigation.

## Tier 2 — secondary (must be labelled)

### USIP — United States Institute of Peace

**The principal Tier 2 source for institutional history.**

- **Michael E. Hartmann**, *International Judges and Prosecutors in Kosovo: A New Model for Post-Conflict Peacekeeping* (USIP Special Report 112, October 2003) — **the principal first-person institutional account**. Hartmann was:
  - First international prosecutor of Kosovo for UNMIK (February 2000 – early 2003)
  - First international public prosecutor before the Supreme Court of Kosovo
  - Previously judicial systems officer for UNMIBH (UN Mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina, 1998-2000)
- Subsequent USIP analytical pieces on Kosovo international judges and prosecutors program

### ECFR — European Council on Foreign Relations

- *International Justice and the Prevention of Atrocities: Case Study on Kosovo* (ecfr.eu) — analytical treatment in comparative perspective, particularly useful for understanding the trajectory from UNMIK Reg. 64 to EULEX to KSC

### OSCE Mission in Kosovo (osce.org/kosovo)

Extensive monitoring and analytical reports on Kosovo judiciary, including:
- *Kosovo: Review of the Criminal Justice System* — periodic editions documenting institutional developments
- Trial monitoring reports
- Reports on access to justice for minority communities

The OSCE Mission in Kosovo has the longest continuous monitoring presence and is **Tier 1-in-practice** for procedural observations on Reg. 64 Panel proceedings.

### Council of Europe

- **Parliamentary Assembly Doc. 9281** (2002) — *Human rights and rule of law in Kosovo* — key analytical reference on Reg. 64 mechanism
- Venice Commission opinions on UNMIK and Kosovo institutional questions
- Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights reports on Kosovo

### ICTJ — International Center for Transitional Justice

- **Tom Perriello and Marieke Wierda**, *Lessons from the Deployment of International Judges and Prosecutors in Kosovo* (ICTJ 2006)
- ICTJ Kosovo-focused reports on the post-conflict judiciary

### Academic literature

- **Hansjörg Strohmeyer**, *Collapse and Reconstruction of a Judicial System: The United Nations Missions in Kosovo and East Timor*, *American Journal of International Law* 95 (2001) 46 — foundational account by an UNTAET/UNMIK legal advisor
- **John Cerone and Clive Baldwin**, *Explaining and Evaluating the UNMIK Court System* in Cesare Romano, André Nollkaemper and Jann Kleffner (eds.), *Internationalized Criminal Courts and Tribunals: Sierra Leone, East Timor, Kosovo and Cambodia* (Oxford University Press 2004)
- **Robert Muharremi**, *The European Union Rule of Law Mission in Kosovo (EULEX) from the Perspective of Kosovo Constitutional Law*, *Zeitschrift für ausländisches öffentliches Recht und Völkerrecht* 70 (2010) 357
- Multiple contributions in *Journal of International Criminal Justice*, *Leiden Journal of International Law*, *International Criminal Law Review*

### NGO and journalistic sources

- **Human Rights Watch** — periodic Kosovo reports
- **Amnesty International** — Kosovo war crimes coverage
- **Balkan Investigative Reporting Network (BIRN)** — balkaninsight.com daily court reporting
- **Hybrid Justice project** — hybridjustice.com comparative analytical resource

## Never authoritative

Wikipedia, Grokipedia, social media, AI summaries.

## A note on languages

Reg. 64 Panel proceedings were conducted in **multiple languages**:
- **Albanian** — primary language of most Kosovo Albanian witnesses, defendants, defence counsel; official language of post-2008 Kosovo
- **Serbian** — primary language of Kosovo Serb participants; official language of Serbia
- **English** — operational language of UNMIK international judges and prosecutors
- **Turkish, Romani** — testimony languages in some cases

Judgments were typically issued in multiple language versions. **For substantive citation, identify the language version cited and the translation chain.**

## A note on archival distribution

Like the SPSC Timor-Leste, Reg. 64 Panel records are **archivally distributed**:
- UNMIK administrative records at unmik.unmissions.org and unmikonline.org (legacy)
- Kosovo court records at Kosovo Judicial Council archives
- EULEX records at eulex-kosovo.eu (for post-2008 successor proceedings)
- legal-tools.org selective mirror
- Personal archives of former international staff
- Academic and NGO archives

This **distributed and partly fragile** archival state is a verification challenge. Anticipate longer verification times than for active institutions; document Tier 1 sources carefully.
