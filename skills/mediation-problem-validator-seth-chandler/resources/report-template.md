## 6. Mediation-Utility Analysis

Address:

- whether law supports or consumes the exercise;
- whether the case is adequately defined;
- genuine bargaining dimensions;
- each BATNA and settlement incentive;
- party balance.

## 7. Settlement-Corridor Analysis

Choose:

- `impossible`
- `brittle`
- `narrow_sound`
- `too_easy`

Describe two to four author-only sound packages. For each, explain authority, financing, linked concessions, implementation, risk control, and comparison with both BATNAs.

Describe at least one tempting but unsound package and why it fails.
