# Exceptional-Need B2G Data Sharing

Use this reference for Chapter V, Articles 14 to 22. Do not treat an ordinary information request, investigation, tax request, or procurement request as a Chapter V request without proving scope.

## 1. Coverage Gate

Article 14 applies where a covered requester demonstrates an Article 15 exceptional need for data and necessary metadata to perform a specific statutory task in the public interest. The responding data holder must be a legal person other than a public sector body and must hold the requested data.

Check Article 16 exclusions and relationships, including criminal or administrative offence activity, penalties, customs, and taxation.

## 2. Article 15 Exceptional Need

### Public emergency, Article 15(1)(a)

The requested data must be necessary to respond to a qualifying public emergency, and the requester must be unable to obtain it by alternative means in a timely and effective manner under equivalent conditions.

### Other public-interest task, Article 15(1)(b)

Only non-personal data may be requested. All conditions must be met:

- requester acts on Union or national law;
- missing specific data prevents a specific public-interest task explicitly provided by law, such as official statistics or mitigation/recovery from a public emergency;
- requester exhausted other means, including market purchase at market rates, existing duties, or legislative measures that could ensure timely availability.

Article 15(1)(b) does not apply to micro and small enterprises, Article 15(2). The market-purchase demonstration has a limited official-statistics exception in Article 15(3).

## 3. Article 17 Request Validation

The written request must be clear, concise, and understandable. Verify that it:

- specifies data and necessary metadata;
- demonstrates the Article 15 ground;
- explains purpose, intended use, duration, and personal-data necessity where relevant;
- gives an expected erasure date if possible;
- justifies selection of this data holder;
- identifies expected recipients;
- specifies necessary personal-data safeguards and anonymisation possibilities;
- identifies the legal provision creating the specific public-interest task;
- gives both the production deadline and the Article 18(2) challenge deadline;
- is specific, proportionate, and limited to data under the holder's control;
- respects legitimate aims, trade secrets, cost, and effort;
- requests personal data only for a public emergency and only if non-personal data are insufficient, then in pseudonymised form;
- states possible Article 40 penalties;
- follows publication, data-coordinator, and data-protection-supervisor steps.

Use the Commission model request template if available, but validate the completed request against Article 17 itself.

## 4. Article 18 Response

The holder must make data available without undue delay, considering necessary technical, organisational, and legal measures.

The holder may decline or seek modification only on an Article 18(2) ground:

- it does not control the data;
- a similar request for the same purpose was submitted and erasure has not been notified;
- the request fails Article 17(1) or (2).

Deadlines to decline or seek modification:

- public-emergency data: without undue delay and no later than five working days after receipt;
- other exceptional need: without undue delay and no later than 30 working days after receipt.

For personal data, anonymise unless compliance requires disclosure of personal data; then pseudonymise, Article 18(4). Disputes that cannot be resolved by modification go to the competent authority under Article 18(5).

Do not invent “manifestly excessive”, commercial-harm, or disproportionate-effort refusal grounds. Burden, proportionality, and commercial interests are addressed through Article 17 validation and request modification, but Article 18(2) contains the operative grounds.

## 5. Recipient Duties and Onward Sharing

Under Article 19, the requester must:

- use data compatibly with the stated purpose;
- preserve confidentiality, integrity, transfer security, and data-subject rights;
- erase data when no longer necessary and notify the holder and Article 21 recipients;
- avoid using data to develop or improve a competing connected product or related service;
- avoid sharing for that competitive purpose;
- use trade secrets only where strictly necessary and implement appropriate safeguards;
- remain responsible for received-data security.

Article 17(4) allows specified exchange with another public body or delegated technical third party, with safeguards and holder notice. Article 21 permits sharing for compatible scientific research or analytics and official statistics, subject to recipient qualifications, equivalent obligations, notice, and the limited six-month retention rule.

## 6. Article 20 Compensation

| Request | Rule |
|---|---|
| Public emergency, non-micro/non-small holder | Free of charge; public acknowledgement on request, Article 20(1) |
| Public emergency, micro or small holder | May claim Article 20(2) compensation via Article 20(3) |
| Article 15(1)(b) request | Technical and organisational compliance costs plus reasonable margin, Article 20(2) |
| Certain official-statistics requests where national law bars purchase | No compensation, Article 20(4) |

The calculation may include anonymisation, pseudonymisation, aggregation, and technical-adaptation costs. The holder must explain the basis when requested. Compensation disputes may be complained about to the Article 37 authority.

## 7. Response File

Maintain:

- original request, delivery evidence, and receipt time;
- requester identity and legal authority;
- Article 14 to 17 validation checklist;
- data inventory and control evidence;
- personal-data, security, and trade-secret assessment;
- response or modification decision and deadline calculation;
- compensation calculation and supporting costs;
- transmitted dataset, metadata, security method, and recipients;
- erasure and onward-sharing notifications;
- authority correspondence and legal review.

## 8. Decision Output

State one result:

- **Valid as submitted:** provide securely and track use/erasure.
- **Modification required:** identify the unmet Article 17 element and propose a compliant narrowing.
- **Decline under Article 18(2):** identify the exact point and evidence.
- **Outside Chapter V:** identify the correct legal route if known, without assuming it is invalid under other law.

Because response periods can be short, escalate the request on receipt. An internal same-day intake target is an implementation recommendation, not the statutory deadline.
