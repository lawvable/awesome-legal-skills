## 7. Article 13 Contract Review

```markdown
| Clause | Covered subject | Negotiation evidence | Article 13 test | Object/effect | Counterevidence | Result | Revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  | 13(4)(a), etc. |  |  |  |  |
```

Checklist:

- [ ] Parties are enterprises.
- [ ] Term concerns covered data, liability, remedy, breach, or termination subject matter.
- [ ] Other party attempted negotiation but could not influence content, or supplier proved otherwise.
- [ ] Mandatory/default Union-law exclusion considered under Article 13(2).
- [ ] General Article 13(3) standard applied.
- [ ] Article 13(4) unfair list checked exactly.
- [ ] Article 13(5) presumed-unfair list checked exactly.
- [ ] Main-subject and price-adequacy exclusion considered.
- [ ] Severability and national-law consequences assessed.
- [ ] Commission model terms treated as non-binding.

## 12. Final Quality Review

- [ ] Facts are distinguished from assumptions.
- [ ] Every legal conclusion has an exact article anchor.
- [ ] Negative legal claims follow a full-provision read.
- [ ] Current consolidated texts and national sources were checked.
- [ ] All dates were independently verified.
- [ ] Technical or contractual evidence supports each compliance claim.
- [ ] Personal-data basis is separate from Data Act entitlement.
- [ ] Findings state confidence and closure evidence.
