### Skill scope at v1.0.0
- Covers the EAC's entire lifecycle (créée 22 août 2012, opérationnelle 8 février 2013 — clôture 27 avril 2017) plus post-EAC reparations execution by AU Trust Fund
- Encodes the same verification-first methodology as the other skills in this repository
- Specifically equips the user to handle the EAC's institutional novelty (first African universal jurisdiction trial; first AU prosecution of an African former Head of State) and the post-Habré-death (24 août 2021) status of the criminal sentence and reparations obligation
- Maps the four core decisions exhaustively (the EAC's documentary corpus is finite — one accused, four decisions, well-bounded body of jurisprudence)

### Known limitations
- The EAC corpus is small and well-mapped; this is a feature, not a bug. The skill covers the entirety of the EAC's substantive output.
- Reparations execution by the AU Trust Fund is **ongoing and minimal**; subsequent developments (asset recovery, voluntary contributions, victim disbursements) will require updating.
- The Chadian national prosecutions of March 2015 (against six DDS officials and others) are **outside the scope of this skill** — they are domestic Chadian proceedings, not EAC proceedings, and should not be conflated.
- The doctrinal debate on the EAC's construction of JCE and command responsibility (Sarah Williams and others) is flagged but not resolved — the skill enables accurate citation of the EAC's findings; the doctrinal debate is left to the user.
